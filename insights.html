New Builds

Microsoft Completes Construction of Saudi Arabia East Cloud Region Ahead of November 2026 Customer Launch, With Three Availability Zones Ready in the Eastern Province

Microsoft confirmed it has completed construction of all three availability zones at its Saudi Arabia East datacenter region, with the region going live to customers in November 2026 and enabling Saudi government agencies and private organisations to host eligible workloads and data locally. The Eastern Province site mirrors the same resilience architecture Microsoft uses across its other Gulf cloud regions in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Riyadh, and sits within the company's broader commitment of more than $10 billion across the Gulf states by 2030.

 Market Intelligence

EQT Seeks Joint AI and Data Center Investments With Saudi and UAE Sovereign Investors, Planning to Expand Through Portfolio Firm EdgeConneX

Europe's largest private markets firm EQT told The National it is in discussions with sovereign investors in Saudi Arabia and the UAE about jointly investing in AI and data center infrastructure, with portfolio company EdgeConneX, which operates more than 106 data centers across 25 countries, expected to serve as the primary vehicle for any new Gulf builds. EQT opened a new office in Abu Dhabi to deepen relationships with sovereign funds, family offices and institutional partners in the region.

Source: The National  ·  3 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Around 2,000 March in Dublin and 1,300 Gather in Helsinki as European Opposition to Data Centers Reaches New Scale Across More Than 15 Countries on 6 and 7 November 2026

Bloomberg reported on 7 November 2026 that some 2,000 people marched in Dublin, according to broadcaster RTE, and around 1,300 gathered in Helsinki as the Stop Dirty Data Centres campaign staged coordinated protests across more than 15 European countries, calling for a moratorium on large-scale data center development. The movement was galvanised by the outcome of the US midterm elections, in which voters in numerous states backed measures restricting data center construction and several anti-data center candidates were elected governor.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  7 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Bank of England Questions UK Lenders Over Exposure to Speculative Data Center Loans Tied to Projects With No Signed Tenants as Financial Stability Concerns Grow

The Bank of England asked lenders in early November 2026 to disclose their exposure to data center projects financed before any tenant has agreed to pay rent, as part of a wider review of lending to the sector, Bloomberg reported on 5 November. The enquiry follows a warning from the Bank's Financial Policy Committee about AI linked financing arrangements it described as carrying leverage, opacity and circular structures, with a survey finding that four lenders had stopped lending to data centers entirely and debt funds supplying almost half of all speculative finance in the sector during the first half of 2026.

Source: Bloomberg / Data Center Dynamics  ·  5 November 2026
 New Builds

Google Commits $15 Billion to Malaysian Data Center Expansion in 2027 and 2028, Adding New Campuses in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan and Pledging 1 GW of Clean Energy

Google announced on 4 November 2026 that it will spend $15 billion expanding its data center infrastructure in Malaysia during 2027 and 2028, with new campuses planned in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan alongside its existing Elmina Business Park facility and a separately committed $160 million for water infrastructure in Negeri Sembilan. As part of the investment Google also pledged to deliver 1 GW of clean energy capacity to Malaysia's national grid.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 November 2026
 New Builds

NTT Data Opens 12 MW AI Ready JKT2A Data Center in Jakarta With Rack Densities of Up to 40 kW and Indonesia's First All Photonics Network Link

NTT Data inaugurated its Jakarta 2 Annex facility on 3 November 2026, a 12 MW, seven storey campus designed to support rack densities of up to 40 kW per rack and built for hyperscale, enterprise and high density AI workloads. The company simultaneously launched its APN Connect service from the new site, which it describes as Indonesia's first All Photonics Network link, connecting JKT2A with the neighbouring JKT3 campus for real-time low latency data synchronisation.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  3 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Newly Elected Governors in Ohio, Georgia, Michigan, New Mexico and Texas Plan to Pause or Stop Data Center Construction, Threatening Around 40 Percent of Total US Planned Capacity

Bloomberg reported on 6 November 2026 that newly elected governors in five states all campaigned on promises of stopping or pausing data center construction, with analyst firm Cleanview estimating that approximately 40 percent of the country's total planned data center capacity sits within those states. A further 18 percent of planned capacity is located in states where incoming governors pledged to revise tax incentives and permitting rules rather than impose an outright moratorium.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  6 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Dominion Energy Reports 54.9 GW of Data Center Demand in Its Virginia Pipeline, Up 13 Percent Year on Year, With 12 GW Already Under Signed Electric Service Agreements

Dominion Energy disclosed in its Q3 2026 earnings report that it holds 54.9 GW of data center capacity requests in various stages of contracting across its Virginia service territory, a 13 percent increase from the end of 2025, with approximately 12 GW already secured under signed electric service agreements. The figures underline how data center power demand continues to outpace the utility's grid investment programme, even as newly elected governors in Virginia and neighbouring states have pledged to slow or pause new data center approvals following the November midterm elections.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  3 November 2026

New Builds

Microsoft Confirms Its Saudi Arabia East Azure Region Will Open in November 2026, Becoming the First Azure Region on Saudi Soil and Completing Its Gulf Cloud Footprint Across All Four Countries in the Region

Microsoft announced on 31 August 2026 that its Saudi Arabia East region, located in the Eastern Province, will become available to customers in November 2026, completing a series of Gulf Azure launches that also cover the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait. The region forms part of the company's commitment to invest more than $10 billion across the four Gulf states through 2030 and is intended to give Saudi government and enterprise customers in country access to cloud and AI services hosted on Saudi soil.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  31 August 2026
 Capex Watch

Dubai's Damac Digital Reveals a 6GW Data Center Landbank Spanning 13 Countries and More Than 35 Sites Across Four Continents, With Construction Under Way on Ten Sites and 700MW of Capacity Targeted for Operation by the First Quarter of 2027

Damac Digital, the data center arm of the Dubai based Damac Group and formerly known as Edgnex, announced at the Datacloud Global Congress in Cannes on 2 June 2026 that its planned IT capacity landbank had reached 6,000MW across sites in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Spain, Italy, Greece, Turkey, the Nordics, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines and the United States. The company, which has five global hyperscaler partnerships in place, is targeting 2GW of operational capacity by the first quarter of 2028, with more than 90% of its planned facilities designed to incorporate liquid cooling for next generation AI workloads.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2 June 2026
 New Builds

CX Tech Files a £16.5 Billion Planning Application for a 1GW Data Center Campus at the Former Chapelcross Nuclear Power Station in Dumfries and Galloway, Scotland, Proposing Eight Buildings Over 55 Hectares to Be Constructed Between 2027 and 2035

CX Tech lodged a planning application in principle with Dumfries and Galloway Council on 28 September 2026 for the CX Technology Campus, comprising eight buildings of approximately 120MW each totalling 162,000 square metres on the site of Scotland's first commercial nuclear power station, validated under reference 26/1649/PIP. The developer proposes to power the campus exclusively from nearby wind, solar and biomass generation via direct and private wire connections, though the application is politically contested as the Scottish Parliament recently voted in favour of pausing decisions on hyperscale data centers until national planning guidance is published.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  30 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

British AI Infrastructure Firm Nscale Raises $3.36 Billion Through Convertible Notes Led by Third Point, With Nvidia, Apollo, Citadel and the Abu Dhabi Investment Council Among the Investors, Ahead of a Planned Nasdaq Listing at a Valuation of Around $30 Billion

Nscale announced on 24 September 2026 that it had secured $3.36 billion in pre IPO convertible financing, with $2.36 billion payable immediately and a further $1 billion committed by Nvidia to follow by mid-November 2026, in a round that also includes Hudson Bay Capital and 8090 Industries. The company reported revenue of $140.6 million for the first half of 2026 and more than $103 billion in total contracted value driven largely by long term agreements with Anthropic and Microsoft, though losses widened to $1.02 billion over the same period.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  24 September 2026
 Capex Watch

Nvidia Backed AI Infrastructure Provider GMI Cloud Closes a $668 Million Round Comprising a $223 Million Series B Led by ARCHIV and a $445 Million Credit Facility Led by CTBC, to Fund Data Center Expansion Across Japan, Taiwan, Singapore and the United States

GMI Cloud, founded in Japan in 2023, announced on 1 October 2026 that it had raised $668 million through a combination of equity and a credit facility, with investors including Nvidia, DSC Investment, Trend Micro, KB Investment, Kyobo Life and KT Corporation participating in the Series B alongside lead investor ARCHIV, a San Francisco based fund focused on AI and robotics. The company said its contracted annual recurring revenue had grown more than ninefold since the end of 2025 to exceed $600 million, and that the proceeds will fund expansion of its data centers and AI inference services across Japan, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan and the United States.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Australia Becomes the First Country to Introduce a Single National Regulatory Framework for Data Centers, Requiring Large Scale Operators to Fund New Clean Energy Supply and Contribute at Least as Much Power to the Grid as They Consume

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced on 15 July 2026 that Australia would introduce binding national standards covering the location, energy sourcing and water use of large data centers, replacing a fragmented approach spanning multiple agencies and levels of government, with legislation targeted at introduction to parliament in early 2027. Under the framework, large scale operators would be required to fund new renewable generation and become net contributors to the grid, with no costs to be passed on to households or businesses, and would be required to minimise water consumption and fund any additional water infrastructure they require.

Source: PV Tech  ·  15 July 2026
 Capex Watch

Akamai Technologies Signs a Seven Year, $11.6 Billion Compute Agreement With Anthropic to Supply CPU Capacity for AI Inference Workloads, With an Option to Expand the Partnership to as Much as $20 Billion and an Equity Warrant Covering Approximately 5% of Akamai's Outstanding Stock

Akamai announced on 24 September 2026 that it had signed a multi year, $11.6 billion contractual commitment with Anthropic, under which Akamai will supply CPU compute capacity to support the AI company's growing inference workloads, with Akamai estimating approximately $5.5 billion in capital expenditure to fulfil the contract. The deal expands on an earlier $1.8 billion arrangement between the two companies and adds to a reported $517 billion in total long term infrastructure commitments Anthropic has signed across data center and compute providers, with Anthropic receiving warrants for up to 5% of Akamai's common stock.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  24 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Goldman Sachs Raises Its End 2026 US Data Center Capacity Forecast by 5GW to 64GW and Projects Power Demand to Grow 38% in Both 2026 and 2027, Concluding That Community Opposition Has Had Limited Near Term Impact on the Sector

Goldman Sachs published a research note on 5 October 2026 raising its estimate for US data center capacity at the end of 2026 from 59GW to 64GW, reflecting stronger than expected project delivery, while trimming its end 2027 forecast by 5GW to 90GW, which the bank described as a timing adjustment rather than a fundamental shift in the long term outlook. Goldman noted that approximately 33% of Americans support the current pace of data center construction, based on a Reuters and Ipsos poll from June 2026, suggesting that community opposition is a political risk but has not yet materially slowed capital deployment across the sector.

Source: US News (Reuters)  ·  5 October 2026

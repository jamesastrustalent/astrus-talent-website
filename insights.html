Market Intelligence

Saudi Arabia's Communications Minister Proposes a Unified Gulf Digital Network at the 30th GCC Ministerial Committee Meeting, Calling for a Shared Data, Computing and AI Ecosystem Modelled on the GCC Electricity Interconnection

Abdullah Alswaha presented the proposal in Bahrain on 18 October 2026, urging member states to pool computing resources and harmonise data sovereignty rules. Saudi Arabia's international network capacity has grown 80% to 18 Tbps, with data center installed capacity rising from 68 MW in 2021 to more than 467 MW in 2026, backed by over $14.9 billion in investment.

Source: Arab News  ·  18 October 2026
 New Builds

Humain Issues a Request for Proposals for a 100 MW AI Data Center Engineering, Procurement and Construction Contract in Riyadh's Al Saad Area Worth an Estimated $1 Billion, With Bids Due 8 November and Phase One Completion Targeted for October 2028

The contract covers a facility designed for rack densities of 240 to 250 kW per rack to support high density GPU clusters, with an optional second phase that would bring total site capacity to 200 MW. The tender marks a continuation of Humain's rapid procurement programme in Saudi Arabia as the kingdom scales its AI infrastructure base.

Source: MEED  ·  18 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Amazon Pledges €16 Billion Investment in Germany for 2027, Including €4.8 Billion for New AWS Data Center and AI Capacity in the Frankfurt Region, as Germany's Economy Minister Calls the Commitment a Vote of Confidence in the Country's Future

Announced on 20 October 2026, the package also covers high speed satellite broadband via Amazon Leo and a Prime Video production slate, adding to the more than €100 billion Amazon has invested in Germany since 2010. AWS already operates a European Sovereign Cloud region in the country, which became generally available in January 2026.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  20 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Finland's Licensing and Supervision Agency Orders Google to Halt All Preparatory Work at Its Muhos and Kajaani AI Data Center Sites by 23 October After Investigators Found Around 300 Hectares of Forest Were Cleared Without a Completed Environmental Impact Assessment

The order, reported on 7 October 2026, bars further ground clearance, road building and earthworks at the two sites, which form part of a projected €13 billion data center buildout across Finland. Google said the logging complied with the Forestry Act but acknowledged the company had fallen short of its own environmental standards.

Source: Al Jazeera  ·  7 October 2026
 Capex Watch

AirTrunk Nears an Approximately US$2 Billion Sale of Its 150 MW Johor Data Center to a Consortium Including Malaysia's KWAP Pension Fund, in a Deal That Would Also Delay Its Planned Singapore Real Estate Investment Trust Float to Early 2027

Reuters reported on 15 October 2026, citing two people familiar with the matter, that Blackstone and CPPIB backed AirTrunk is close to agreeing terms for the sale of around 80% of the equity in its JHB1 facility. The transaction comes as AI linked equities have sold off and interest rates have risen, testing the pace of deal activity across the Asia Pacific data center market.

Source: Reuters  ·  15 October 2026
 New Builds

Malaysia's Data Centre Task Force Approves 27 Projects Worth RM225.97 Billion (Approximately US$55 Billion), With 16 Facilities in Johor, 10 in Selangor and One in Negeri Sembilan, Together Expected to Create More Than 4,000 Jobs

Reported by Bernama on 14 October 2026, the approvals cover 26 facilities still under construction with one already operational, all subject to six monthly progress reports filed with the Malaysia Investment Development Authority. Digital Minister Gobind Singh Deo told Parliament that 84 data center projects have been approved nationwide as of July 2026.

Source: Bernama  ·  14 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Amazon Withdraws Its Application for an 800,000 Square Foot Data Center Campus at the Former George Washington University Site in Ashburn, Virginia After Community Opposition, Instead Offering 25 Acres for a Community Arts Centre and Sports Facility

Announced by Loudoun County Board Chair Phyllis Randall on 20 October 2026, the reversal on the Bridgefield Tech Center plan resolves a dispute over the 122 acre site Amazon bought for $427 million, with the remainder to be rezoned for mixed use development with housing as the principal use. The withdrawal is a significant setback for data center expansion in one of the world's most concentrated data center corridors.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  20 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Virginia Senator Russet Perry Introduces Senate Bill 843 Proposing a Three Year Statewide Data Center Moratorium That Would Halt New State Approvals From July 2027, Giving Legislators Time to Develop a Permanent Regulatory Framework for the Industry

Reported by Data Center Dynamics on 15 October 2026, the bill targets the rapid expansion that has made Loudoun County in Northern Virginia the world's largest data center market, where a separate local 12 month pause is also under consideration. New York became the first US state to impose a statewide data center moratorium in July 2026, pausing environmental permits for projects of 50 MW or more.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  15 October 2026

Market Intelligence

Humain and Saudi Arabia's National Infrastructure Fund Sign $667 Million in Financing Agreements to Fund Power Infrastructure for the Kingdom's AI Data Center Programme

Humain and the National Infrastructure Fund (Infra) have signed two financing agreements worth up to SR2.5 billion ($667 million), marking the first transactions under Infra's AI Data Center Financing Programme and including a SR1.4 billion deal to fund substations, transmission lines and grid connections for Humain's Saudi data centers. The agreements build on the $1.2 billion non binding framework announced at Davos in January 2026 and form part of Saudi Arabia's accelerating push to establish domestic AI infrastructure capacity under Project Transcendence.

Source: Arab News  ·  November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Data Center World Makes Its Gulf Debut in Abu Dhabi on 17 and 18 November, Gathering Regional Governments, Operators and AI Infrastructure Leaders to Address Power, Cooling and Capacity Challenges

The inaugural Middle East edition of Data Center World opens in Abu Dhabi on 17 November 2026, with the UAE Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure, Khazna Data Centers, Core42, AWS, Google and NVIDIA among participants, covering power, cooling and AI workload challenges across UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar and Bahrain. The event reflects Abu Dhabi's position as the region's largest data center market, with JLL reporting 374 megawatts of IT capacity in the city as of mid 2026.

Source: Channel Post MEA  ·  12 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

"Stop Dirty Data Centres" Days of Action Span 16 European Countries on 6 and 7 November, With Protesters Entering a Microsoft Site in Amsterdam and Thousands Marching in Dublin and Helsinki

More than 100 organisations in 16 countries took part in the Stop Dirty Data Centres days of action on 6 and 7 November 2026, with protesters entering a Microsoft construction site in Amsterdam, around 2,000 people marching in Dublin and approximately 1,300 gathering in Helsinki, all calling for an immediate moratorium on new large scale data center development across the continent. The campaign followed US midterm results in which voters restricted data center construction in 40 of 41 local ballot measures, and comes as the European Commission advances plans to at least treble the bloc's data center capacity within five to seven years.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  5 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Finland Orders Google to Halt Preparatory Work at Two Data Center Sites After Contractors Cleared More Than 330 Hectares of Protected Forest Without the Required Environmental Assessments

Finland's environmental supervisory authority ordered Google's project company to stop work at the Muhos and Kajaani sites in October 2026 until mandatory environmental impact assessments are completed, following the clearance of approximately 330 hectares of forest that exceeded the 200 hectare threshold requiring formal assessment under Finnish law. Environment Minister Sari Multala said the projects face delays and Google acknowledged that it may have missed internal sustainability benchmarks, though the company denied deliberate wrongdoing.

Source: Al Jazeera  ·  October 2026

Princeton Digital Group Secures $1.3 Billion in Green Financing From Six Banks for Its 200 Megawatt AI and Cloud Data Center Campus in Johor, Malaysia

Singapore headquartered Princeton Digital Group raised $1.3 billion in green financing for its JH1 campus at Sedenak Tech Park in Johor from Affin Bank, HSBC, Maybank, OCBC, Standard Chartered and UOB, as part of a broader plan to raise up to $5 billion in debt to fund contracted data center capacity across Asia Pacific. The 200 megawatt campus is designed for high density AI and cloud workloads and holds Green Building Index Gold and LEED Gold certifications, with the green loan tied to those environmental standards.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Australia's NextDC Pauses All Uncontracted Data Center Projects After Power Delays and Community Backlash at Its Melbourne M3 Site Send the Company's Share Price Down 42 Per Cent

NextDC announced on 9 November 2026 that it will suspend work on all data center projects without customer contracts in place, following the forced suspension of its Melbourne M3 expansion after power delays and local opposition, which triggered a forecast cut in October and a 42 per cent decline in its share price. CEO Craig Scroggie said construction cannot restart until the company has clearly communicated that the underlying problems are resolved, and Goodman Group and Digital Realty subsequently acquired a 9.9 per cent stake in NextDC following the share price fall.

Source: Reuters  ·  9 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

US Voters Back Restrictions on Data Centers in 40 Out of 41 Local Ballot Measures on 3 November, With Bans, Moratoriums and Energy Caps Passing Across Ohio, Illinois and Eight Other States

American voters approved restrictions on data center development in 40 of 41 local ballot measures on 3 November 2026, with measures ranging from outright bans above a size threshold to temporary moratoriums and energy consumption caps, in a nationwide vote on the AI infrastructure boom that analysts said contributed to a wider shift in the midterm results. Ohio had the most measures on the ballot with 21, followed by Illinois with 7, and the sole exception in favour of data center development was in Harwood, North Dakota.

Source: The Washington Post  ·  4 November 2026

Google Commits $17 Billion to Its First Wyoming Data Center Campus Near Cheyenne, Powered by 702 Megawatts of Fuel Cell Generation With Carbon Capture on a 716 Acre Site in Laramie County

Google announced on 5 November 2026 that it will invest $17 billion in a data center campus in Laramie County, Wyoming, powered by a 702 megawatt fuel cell plant operated with American Electric Power and designed to avoid drawing on the local water supply through air cooling. The campus, known internally as Project Tembo, is planned to reach full operation by 2031 with the power facility targeted for completion by end of 2028, and will generate hundreds of millions of dollars in property taxes for Laramie County schools.

Source: Google  ·  5 November 2026

Gulf States Press Ahead With AI Data Center Buildout as Stargate UAE Targets First 200 Megawatt Tranche in the Third Quarter of 2026

State backed firms across the Gulf are continuing to advance large scale AI infrastructure programmes despite data centers becoming repeated targets in the conflict between the United States and Iran. Stargate UAE, the G42 led project in Abu Dhabi, is targeting commissioning of its first 200 megawatt capacity tranche in the third quarter of 2026, with structural work advanced and the first critical mechanical systems already on site, as operators absorb the additional cost of enhanced security measures and more dispersed facility siting across the region.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  4 September 2026

PIF Backed Humain Plans Initial $2.5 Billion Fund to Finance 250 Megawatt of AI Data Center Capacity in Saudi Arabia With Potential to Scale to One Gigawatt

Saudi AI firm Humain is raising an initial $2.5 billion from global and domestic investors for a fund managed by BSF Capital, blending debt and equity to finance 250 megawatts of AI data center capacity in partnership with Al Moammar Information Systems, with expansion rights to one gigawatt. The fundraise forms part of a broader buildout targeting 1.9 gigawatts of AI compute across Saudi Arabia by 2030 and over six gigawatts by 2034.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  3 September 2026
 New Builds

Nabiax Breaks Ground on €800 Million Alcalá Data Center 3 in Madrid, Adding 100 Megawatt of IT Capacity by 2029 and Taking Spanish Portfolio to 135 Megawatt

Spanish data center operator Nabiax held a cornerstone ceremony in Alcalá de Henares on 21 September for its third Madrid campus, ADC3, representing a nearly €800 million investment that will add up to 100 megawatts of IT capacity through 2029 using a phased build with closed loop cooling. The project lifts Nabiax's total installed IT power in Spain to 135 megawatts across its Madrid and Catalonia sites, with the new facility designed to serve hyperscale and AI customers.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  21 September 2026

NorthC Breaks Ground on 6 Megawatt FRA01 Data Center at Mainzer Landstraße in West Frankfurt, With First Capacity Targeted for January 2028

Dutch operator NorthC has commenced preparatory construction work on its FRA01 data center in Frankfurt, a 6 megawatt colocation facility at Mainzer Landstraße 695 in the west of the city, with first capacity expected in January 2028. The groundbreaking is the latest step in NorthC's German expansion strategy, which encompasses multiple further facilities in development across the Frankfurt metropolitan area alongside an existing site acquired from Colt, as the operator deepens its presence in one of Europe's most sought after colocation markets.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  17 September 2026

New South Wales Grants Planning Consent for Amazon's AUD 2.17 Billion, 235 Megawatt Data Center Campus at Glendenning in Western Sydney

The New South Wales Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure has approved a major Amazon Web Services data center campus at 2 Glendenning Road in Sydney's west, comprising three five storey buildings with a combined IT capacity of 193.6 megawatts and a peak electrical demand of 235 megawatts, at an estimated total cost of AUD 2.17 billion. The consent, granted on 14 September 2026, covers the redevelopment of a 10.4 hectare brownfield site into one of the state's largest single digital infrastructure investments, to be delivered across three staged phases.

Source: w.media  ·  14 September 2026

Bitdeer AI Signs 10 Year Services Agreement for 65.1 Megawatt Liquid Cooled AI Data Center at Johor Campus, Lifting Malaysian Footprint to 86.8 Megawatt

Bitdeer AI has entered a 10 year data center services agreement for A202, a 65.1 megawatt liquid cooled facility on its existing Johor Bahru campus alongside the 21.7 megawatt A201 building, with energisation expected in the third quarter of 2027. The facility will host Nvidia GB300 and NVL72 hardware and takes Bitdeer AI's total secured capacity across Malaysia, Norway and the United States to approximately 206.5 megawatts, as the operator works toward a stated target of 350 megawatts by early 2028.

Source: Bitdeer  ·  14 September 2026

Research Finds 45 US Data Center Projects Worth $68 Billion Were Blocked or Delayed by Community Opposition in the Second Quarter of 2026

Research group Data Center Watch has found that 45 data center projects with a combined value of $68 billion were blocked or delayed by community opposition in the United States between April and June 2026, as localities pushed through moratoriums on new construction ahead of planning applications and 30 state legislatures introduced or adopted rules on data center siting, energy usage and water consumption. There are now 843 organised opposition groups operating across 49 states, with one petition against a Tennessee project gathering more than 500,000 signatures during the quarter alone.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  21 September 2026

Aligned Data Centers Breaks Ground on 2 Gigawatt Project Phoenix Campus at Former Power Station Site in Shippingport, Pennsylvania, Anchoring $10 Billion Regional Investment

Aligned Data Centers has held a groundbreaking ceremony for Project Phoenix, a planned 2 gigawatt hyperscale AI data center campus on the 660 acre Shippingport Industrial Park in Beaver County, Pennsylvania, adjacent to the former Shippingport Power Station, with an estimated total economic investment of $10 billion. The campus will be powered by a dedicated on site natural gas generation facility rather than the public grid, and is expected to support 3,000 construction jobs and 640 permanent operational roles, with surplus capacity to be returned to the PJM grid serving the northeastern United States.

Source: GlobeNewswire  ·  10 September 2026

UAE Revises Plans for Five Gigawatt AI Campus Into Distributed Network of Facilities After Iranian Attacks

The United Arab Emirates is quietly revising its plans for a five gigawatt AI data center project, originally conceived as a single ten square mile campus in Abu Dhabi, into a distributed network of facilities spread across the Emirates following sustained Iranian drone and missile attacks on regional cloud infrastructure. The G42 led project, one of the largest AI infrastructure programmes outside the United States and controlled by UAE national security adviser Sheikh Tahnoun bin Zayed al Nahyan, will incorporate additional protection measures including the potential for underground construction at some facilities and enhanced air defences.

Source: Reuters  ·  11 September 2026

AWS and Humain Confirm 50 Megawatt Saudi AI Zone Target for 2028 as Kingdom's First Cloud Region Nears December Launch

Amazon Web Services and the Public Investment Fund backed Humain have confirmed that the first 50 megawatts of capacity at the Saudi AI Zone in Riyadh will be live by 2028, with approximately 150,000 AI chips including Nvidia GB300 accelerators and AWS Trainium processors set to be deployed in a combined investment exceeding $5.3 billion. AWS's first dedicated cloud infrastructure region in Saudi Arabia, offering three availability zones for customers across the Kingdom, remains on track to open in December 2026.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  5 September 2026

Digital Realty Forms Joint Venture With Rönesans Infrastructure to Develop Data Centers Across Türkiye

Digital Realty has formed a strategic joint venture with Rönesans Infrastructure to develop and operate next generation data centers across Türkiye, with the first campus to be a carrier neutral facility in Ankara designed to support more than 22 megawatts of IT capacity, targeted for completion in 2028. The partnership plans to expand into Istanbul following the Ankara opening, positioning Türkiye as a key digital hub at the intersection of European, Middle Eastern and Asian connectivity.

Source: GlobeNewswire  ·  14 September 2026

HSCALE, Backed by Bain Capital, Wins Over $1 Billion Contract With a Major US Cloud Provider for Its Spanish Data Center Campus

HSCALE, the EMEA focused data center platform launched by Bain Capital in 2025, has secured a contract worth more than $1 billion with an unnamed major US cloud provider for capacity at one of Spain's largest data center campuses currently under construction, with the first capacity available to the client in 2027. Spain's position as a gateway between European, African and Latin American digital markets has made it one of the most active data center development stories in Europe during 2026.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  9 September 2026

DayOne Breaks Ground on 42 Megawatt Kodaira Campus in Tokyo as Japan Overtakes India to Become Asia Pacific's Second Largest Data Center Market

DayOne, the Asia Pacific focused data center operator spun out of Chinese firm GDS in 2025, has broken ground on Phase I of its Kodaira Data Center Campus west of Tokyo, with the first building set to deliver 15 megawatts of IT capacity and the full two building campus targeting a total of 42 megawatts. The groundbreaking comes as Japan added 293 megawatts of operational capacity in 2026, lifting its total to 1.8 gigawatts and overtaking India to become the second largest operational data center market in Asia Pacific behind mainland China.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  14 September 2026

Bitdeer AI Secures 65.1 Megawatt AI Cloud Facility at Its Johor Bahru Campus in Malaysia Under a 10 Year Agreement

Bitdeer AI has entered into a 10 year data center services agreement for A202, a 65.1 megawatt AI cloud facility at its existing Johor Bahru campus in Malaysia, with the building designed to host liquid cooled rack scale NVIDIA GB300 and Vera Rubin systems and energisation targeted for the third quarter of 2027. The deal brings the campus to a combined 86.8 megawatts of critical IT load alongside the existing A201 facility, lifting Bitdeer's total secured AI cloud capacity to approximately 207 megawatts across Malaysia, Norway and the United States as its active AI cloud pipeline surpasses $7 billion.

Source: GlobeNewswire  ·  14 September 2026
 Capex Watch

Crusoe Raises $3.9 Billion Series F at $30.9 Billion Valuation to Expand Its Vertically Integrated AI Infrastructure Platform

Crusoe has closed the initial tranche of a $3.9 billion Series F funding round at a $30.9 billion post money valuation, co led by Atreides Management, Mubadala Capital and Valor Equity Partners, with backing from Founders Fund, GIC, Nvidia, the Qatar Investment Authority and TPG. The capital will fund expansion of Crusoe's AI factory network across large scale hyperscale campuses and modular Crusoe Spark units; the company now reports over one gigawatt of operational capacity and more than $140 billion in total contracted value across its vertically integrated platform.

Source: GlobeNewswire  ·  17 September 2026

CoreWeave Signs 15 Year Anchor Lease at Blockfusion's Hydroelectric Powered Niagara Falls AI Campus in New York

North East Data, the Blockfusion subsidiary developing a former power station at Niagara Falls in New York, has signed a definitive 15 year anchor lease with CoreWeave, converting a non-binding letter of intent that had contemplated 85 megawatts of guaranteed take or pay capacity expandable to 300 megawatts and lease payments of up to $2.8 billion over the initial term. The campus will support high density liquid cooled AI infrastructure and is powered predominantly by hydroelectric generation, positioning it among the most energy efficient large scale AI facilities under development in the United States.

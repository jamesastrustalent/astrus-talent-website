Market Intelligence

US Threat to Tax Overseas AI Data Center Builds Could Push Gulf States Toward Home Grown Capability, as HUMAIN Expands Its Nvidia Partnership and Analysts Argue the Measure May Deepen Rather Than Disrupt American Technology Relationships in the Region

Reporting published today in The National argues that President Trump's threatened tax on US companies building data centers outside the United States may push Saudi Arabia and the UAE to accelerate their own AI capacity rather than wait on foreign funded builds. HUMAIN, the Public Investment Fund backed AI company, recently expanded its Nvidia partnership to cover an 84MW AI computing project, and analysts told the paper that the administration's measure could ultimately encourage Gulf states to build sovereign computing capability as a hedge against US regulatory uncertainty.

Source: The National  ·  26 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Microsoft Opens Its Saudi Arabia East Datacenter Region This Month, Completing a Project Three Years in the Making That Delivers Three Availability Zones and Local Data Residency to Enterprise and Government Customers Under Saudi Vision 2030

Microsoft confirmed at the LEAP conference in Riyadh in August that its Saudi Arabia East datacenter region would open to customers in November, completing a project first announced in 2023 and built to three availability zones in the Eastern Province. The region is designed to allow Saudi government entities and private organisations to run Microsoft cloud and AI services with local data residency, low latency and enterprise security standards aligned with Saudi Vision 2030 digital sovereignty goals.

Source: Microsoft News  ·  November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Spain Publishes a Royal Decree Requiring Data Centers to Source 80 Per Cent of Their Electricity From Renewables Matched Hour by Hour, as Aragón Launches a Supreme Court Challenge and Google Pauses a Major Campus Ahead of a General Election on 29 November

Royal Decree 1032/2026, published in Spain's official gazette on 21 November, requires all data centers to match 80 per cent of their electricity consumption with renewables on an hourly basis, with a five year transition period for existing facilities. Aragón's regional government, which has attracted Microsoft, Amazon and other hyperscalers to what analysts describe as the country's leading data center cluster with more than €60 billion of planned investment, has filed a Supreme Court challenge and called the requirements unmanageable, while the opposition People's Party has pledged to repeal the decree if it wins the election.

Source: CNBC  ·  17 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Quantitative Trading Firm XTX Markets Opens Its First Owned Data Center in Kajaani, Finland, With 22.5MW of Initial Capacity on a 193 Hectare Campus Earmarked for More Than €1 Billion in Total Investment and a Second Building Due in 2027

XTX Markets launched its inaugural data center in Kajaani in late November, taking an unusual step for a financial services company by owning and operating its own large scale compute campus rather than leasing space from a commercial provider. The site, built by construction firm YIT on a 193 hectare plot in northern Finland, adds to a growing cluster of hyperscale and specialist facilities in the region drawn by low temperatures, low electricity prices and renewable power availability.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Fitch Ratings Warns That US Export Controls Effective 19 November Will Slow and Differentiate Asia Pacific Data Center Investment, With Neocloud Operators Carrying the Greatest Risk Due to Their Dependence on Advanced GPUs and High Utilisation Requirements

Fitch Ratings said on 26 November that the new US rules requiring licences for advanced chip shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia will make Asia Pacific data center investment more selective, with neocloud operators most exposed because they rely on costly graphics processors that must be kept at high utilisation to remain commercially viable. Cloud providers in the four affected markets have a 90 day window to obtain initial licences, and Fitch expects project financing to tighten on deployments where chip supply or customer eligibility is uncertain.

Source: The Star / Reuters  ·  26 November 2026

NTT Global Data Centers and Amata B.Grimm Power Sign a Memorandum of Understanding to Study a 400MW Hyperscale Data Center Campus in Chonburi, Thailand, With Investment Estimated at More Than USD 3.3 Billion

NTT Global Data Centers and Amata B.Grimm Power signed a memorandum of understanding on 19 November to jointly study a 400MW hyperscale campus at the Amata City Chonburi Industrial Estate in Thailand, at a planned investment of more than THB 113 billion, equivalent to approximately USD 3.3 billion. The agreement was signed at the Thailand Japan Investment and Business Dialogue in Tokyo and builds on a 100MW power purchase agreement the two parties concluded in January 2026 for the NTT Bangkok 4 campus in Chonburi.

Source: The Tech Capital  ·  24 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

The US Army Invites Private Proposals to Develop AI Data Centers Across Nine Military Sites on the Condition That Every Bid Includes On Site Power Generation, Extending a Federal Push to Mobilise Private Capital Inside Secure Government Perimeters

The US Army released a request for proposals covering nine military sites where private companies may bid to develop and operate AI data centers, with on site power generation mandatory in all submissions and power only bids also accepted. The initiative follows a comparable offer by NASA at Ames Research Center and reflects a federal strategy to draw private AI investment onto existing government land where security perimeters and potential grid connections reduce development costs and timelines.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  25 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Blackstone's Newly Listed Digital Infrastructure Trust Agrees to Pay USD 5 Billion for a 75 Per Cent Stake in Two Amazon Web Services Joint Ventures Holding 647MW Across 13 Powered Shell Data Center Buildings in Northern Indiana and Mississippi

Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust announced on 16 November the acquisition of a 75 per cent interest in two AWS joint ventures owning 13 recently built powered shell buildings with 647MW of IT capacity, funded with USD 1.9 billion of IPO proceeds alongside approximately USD 2.5 billion of project level mortgage debt secured against the properties. Amazon Web Services retains a 25 per cent stake and will lease the portfolio under triple net agreements with an initial 12 year term, an annual rent escalation of 2.75 per cent, and a lease guarantee from Amazon.com.

Source: REBusiness Online  ·  16 November 2026

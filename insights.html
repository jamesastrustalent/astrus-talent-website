Market Intelligence

Saudi Aramco Closes a $2.6 Billion Deal to Acquire 49 Per Cent of AI Company HUMAIN From the Public Investment Fund, Transferring Cash and AI Assets Including a 19,000 Chip Groq Inference Data Center

Aramco paid the Public Investment Fund approximately $2.6 billion in cash on 1 November 2026 and transferred AI assets including a 19,000 chip Groq inference data center in return for a 49 per cent stake in HUMAIN, while the PIF retains majority control. HUMAIN is targeting 1.7 gigawatts of data center capacity by 2027 and 3 gigawatts by 2030, with the ambition of positioning Saudi Arabia as a global exporter of AI compute.

Source: Saudi Public Investment Fund  ·  1 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Iran Linked Ships Are Reported Circling Subsea Cables in the Gulf of Oman as Tehran Repeats Threats Against Data Center and Cloud Infrastructure Operated by US Technology Firms Across the Gulf States

Maritime analytics firm Windward tracked at least four vessels linked to Iran taking unusual routes and loitering above subsea cables in the Gulf of Oman, following Iranian security officials warning that digital infrastructure owned by US technology companies in the region could be targeted if conflict resumes, according to a Financial Times report cited by Data Center Dynamics. AWS has separately said it exhausted every option to restore access to some data hosted in Bahrain following disruption to Gulf cloud infrastructure earlier in 2026.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  6 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Around 2,000 March in Dublin and 1,300 Gather in Helsinki as the Stop Dirty Data Centres Campaign Stages Coordinated Demonstrations at Approximately 85 Locations Across 16 European Countries

Protesters rallied outside data center facilities and construction sites in Dublin, Helsinki, Berlin, Paris and dozens of other cities on 7 November 2026, as the Stop Dirty Data Centres campaign, backed by Friends of the Earth, Greenpeace and Foxglove, coordinated actions across Europe in the week after US voters backed restrictions on data center construction in 38 of 39 finalised local ballot measures. Organisers demanded a moratorium on new approvals, citing energy and water consumption alongside military and law enforcement contracts held by some operators.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  7 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Finnish Police Open a Criminal Investigation Into Google's Muhos Data Center Site After Construction Clearances Were Carried Out Before a Mandatory Environmental Impact Assessment Had Been Completed

The Oulu Police Department launched a criminal investigation on 29 October 2026 into Tuike, the Finnish company representing Google, following preparatory works at the Muhos site in northern Finland that cleared forest without first completing the required environmental assessment, while Finnish regional authorities had already ordered all construction halted at both the Muhos and Kajaani sites in early October. Google apologised for the clearances and said it believed the works complied with Finnish law, adding that it was cooperating with the investigation.

Source: The Next Web  ·  30 October 2026
 New Builds

Equinix Opens Its $290 Million SG6 Data Center in Singapore With Liquid Cooling for AI and High Performance Compute, Becoming the First Facility Built Under Singapore's Data Centre Call for Application Framework

The first phase of SG6 adds 8 megawatts of capacity across approximately 1,550 cabinets and supports high density racks for AI and HPC workloads with liquid cooling, targeting a power usage effectiveness of 1.20 and BCA IMDA Green Mark Platinum certification, as Equinix confirmed it has maintained 100 per cent renewable energy coverage across its Singapore sites since 2020. The company plans a joint innovation facility with the National University of Singapore at the same campus, targeted for 2027.

Source: Fintech News Singapore  ·  6 November 2026
 New Builds

NTT Data Opens Its 12 Megawatt Jakarta 2 Annex Data Center in Indonesia With Tier III Design and a New Low Latency Link to the Larger JKT3 Campus in Bekasi

JKT2A delivers 12 megawatts of IT capacity across 5,800 square metres of space at NTT Data's South Jakarta campus and supports racks up to 40 kilowatts, built to Tier III design standards and completing a project first announced in 2024. NTT Data also launched APN Connect, a dedicated low latency interconnect between JKT2A and its Bekasi campus, as it moves to meet rising demand for AI and cloud infrastructure across Southeast Asia.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  3 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

NTT Data Signs Its Largest Power Agreement, Commissioning 900 Megawatts of Bloom Energy Solid Oxide Fuel Cell Systems as Primary Power for Its Global Data Center Portfolio in a Deal Bloom Calls Its Biggest International Contract to Date

NTT Global Data Centers will deploy Bloom Energy fuel cells as the primary power source across several new data center projects globally, with initial shipments due to begin in 2027 and specific site locations and financial terms not yet disclosed, as the companies announced the agreement on 5 November 2026. Bloom Energy shares rose 10 per cent on the news, which follows a separate deal between Bloom and Oracle covering potential purchases of up to 2.8 gigawatts of fuel cell capacity.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  5 November 2026

SpaceXAI Files Plans for a 6 Gigawatt Data Center Campus Across Nine Buildings on 747 Acres in Cochise County, Arizona, Paired With a 24 Gigawatt Hour Battery Storage System to Protect the State Grid From Demand Spikes

County records filed by Tombstone Resources LLC, which engineers attribute to SpaceXAI, describe a campus the company says could reach 6 gigawatts at full build and which would be by far the largest data center site in the world if approved, with $3.6 billion in planned power infrastructure and air cooling technology rather than water. A zoning decision is scheduled for 9 December 2026, as the application arrives in the same week that US voters backed new restrictions on data center construction across 38 of 39 counted local ballot measures.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  5 November 2026

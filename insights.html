Market Intelligence

Microsoft Saudi Arabia East Datacenter Region Set to Open to Customers in November 2026, Bringing Azure Cloud Services to Saudi Organisations for the First Time

Microsoft confirmed in August 2026 that its Saudi Arabia East datacenter region, situated in the Eastern Province and built across three availability zones, would become available to customers this month, enabling government and commercial organisations to run eligible cloud workloads locally without routing data abroad. The launch expands Microsoft's global cloud footprint to 70 regions across 33 countries and follows more than three years of construction activity in the kingdom.

Source: Microsoft / Data Center Dynamics  ·  November 2026

Saudi Arabia's GO Telecom Awards SAR 185.9 Million Contract to Build AI Cloud Data Center Equipped With Dell Technologies Servers and NVIDIA GPUs

Etihad GO Telecom awarded Total Technical Triangle a SAR 185.9 million contract on 29 October 2026 to construct a cloud AI data center with Dell Technologies equipment and NVIDIA GPU accelerators, with the build scheduled to complete within 12 months; GO plans to use the facility to offer AI and cloud computing services to public and private sector customers across Saudi Arabia.

Source: Zawya / Reuters  ·  1 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

atNorth Signs Agreement With Calentix to Supply Surplus Heat From Its 300 MW Data Center Campus in Sollefteå to the Långsele District Heating Network

atNorth has agreed with infrastructure developer Calentix to pipe waste heat from the SWE04 data center campus in Sollefteå, northern Sweden, to the Långsele district heating network via between two and ten kilometres of new pipeline, with first heat delivery targeted for 2029. Calentix will develop and finance the project, which adds to atNorth's growing portfolio of heat reuse schemes across the Nordic countries.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Portugal Drafts Legislation Requiring Data Centers to Source All Power From Renewable Energy and to Adopt Waterless Cooling Under New Sustainability Rules

The Portuguese government has circulated draft legislation that would require data center operators to run entirely on renewable electricity and to adopt waterless cooling technologies, joining neighbouring Spain in moving towards mandatory sustainability requirements for the sector. The rules are not yet in force and the government is continuing consultation with the industry before finalising the legislation.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  November 2026
 New Builds

NTT Data Opens JKT2A Data Center in Jakarta, a 12 MW Seven Storey Facility Designed for High Density AI Workloads With Up to 40 kW Per Rack

NTT Data opened the JKT2A data center on its Jakarta campus on 3 November 2026, a seven storey building providing 12 MW across 5,800 square metres with rack densities of up to 40 kW per rack and a new low latency APN Connect link to the adjacent JKT3 campus; the site is planned to reach 45 MW when the full campus is complete.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  3 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Malaysia Requires New Data Centers to Source at Least 30 Percent of Their Power From Renewable Energy Under Rules Announced in Parliament

Malaysia's Deputy Prime Minister Fadillah Yusof announced on 29 October 2026 that all new data center operators must procure a minimum of 30 percent of their electricity from renewable sources, with the Energy Commission drafting a compliance framework and operators encouraged to install onsite solar generation. The government separately approved a further 5 GW of data center capacity conditional on operators meeting the renewable energy threshold.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  29 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Google Provides Credit Backstop for TeraWulf's $19 Billion Anthropic Data Center Lease in Hawesville, Kentucky, Covering 401 MW of Computing Capacity

Google LLC agreed on 4 November 2026 to provide contractual credit support for the project financing underpinning TeraWulf's data center development in Hawesville, Kentucky, where Anthropic has signed a 20 year, $19 billion lease covering 401 MW of capacity; TeraWulf simultaneously agreed a definitive engineering, procurement and construction contract with Fluor, with initial capacity expected online in the second half of 2027.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Dominion Energy Discloses 54.9 GW of Data Center Capacity in Contracting Stages in Northern Virginia, Up 13 Percent Since the End of 2025

Dominion Energy's third quarter 2026 earnings disclosed 54.9 GW of data center capacity in various stages of contracting across its Virginia service territory, with 12 GW already under signed electrical service agreements, representing a 13 percent increase since the end of last year. The figures from the utility that serves Northern Virginia, home to the world's largest concentration of data centers, underline the continued strength of AI infrastructure demand in the region.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  3 November 2026

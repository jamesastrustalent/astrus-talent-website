Market Intelligence

Trends, analysis, and perspectives from inside the Data Center talent market.

Last updated 16 October 2026

Geopolitical Risk

Iran Warns It Will Target Gulf Data Centers and Undersea Cables if War With the US Resumes, Following Earlier Drone Strikes That Left AWS Unable to Restore Data From Its UAE Availability Zone

Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei warned on 13 October 2026 that data centers, AI facilities and undersea cables across the UAE and the Strait of Hormuz would be attacked if conflict with the United States resumed. Earlier drone strikes this year damaged two AWS UAE data centers and a Bahrain facility, and Amazon Web Services has since confirmed it cannot restore resources hosted in its UAE availability zone mec1-az2, marking the first known permanent cloud data loss attributable to a conflict.

Source: Al Jazeera  ·  13 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

S&P Projects UAE and Saudi Arabia Will Account for Over 80% of Gulf Data Center Capacity Growth to 2030, With Combined IT Power Additions of Up to 3GW and Construction Costs of $11 to $13 Million Per Megawatt

S&P Global Ratings forecasts the UAE and Saudi Arabia will together add 2 to 3GW of IT power capacity by 2030, representing more than 80% of GCC regional growth, with the UAE moving faster and PIF backed HUMAIN targeting 1.9GW of Saudi capacity by 2030 before scaling to 6.6GW by 2034. S&P cautioned that hardening facilities against physical threats following Iran linked strikes could add 5 to 7% to construction costs across the region.

Source: Khaleej Times / S&P Global Ratings  ·  5 October 2026
 Sustainability

Google Faces Calls to Halt Its €13 Billion Finland Data Center Programme After Roughly 300 Hectares of Forest Were Cleared at the Muhos Site Ahead of Completed Environmental Impact Assessments

Google's data center expansion in Finland is under investigation after the company reportedly cleared roughly 300 hectares of forest at its Muhos site before mandatory environmental assessments were complete. Both the Finnish Association for Nature Conservation and the Safety and Chemicals Agency have called for construction to stop pending review, and local plans at two municipalities have been appealed to the administrative court, placing a building ban across those areas.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  October 2026
 Capex Watch

UK AI Cloud Operator Nscale Secures $3.36 Billion in Pre IPO Convertible Notes Led by Third Point, With Nvidia Committing a Further $1 Billion in November and the Company Targeting a $35 Billion NYSE Valuation

UK headquartered AI cloud platform and data center operator Nscale announced on 25 September 2026 that it had secured $3.36 billion in pre IPO convertible financing, with $2.36 billion payable at closing and a further $1 billion committed by Nvidia due in mid November. The company reported more than $103 billion in total contracted value and is reportedly targeting a $35 billion valuation ahead of a proposed New York Stock Exchange listing.

Source: The Tech Capital  ·  25 September 2026
 Capex Watch

DayOne Raises $3.67 Billion in New Malaysian Financing From Seven Banks Including DBS and Standard Chartered, Bringing Its Total Malaysia Facility to $7.2 Billion as Signed Bookings Reach 2.3GW

Singapore based data center developer DayOne secured an additional RM15 billion ($3.67 billion) in financing from a consortium of seven banks on 13 October 2026, structured as a dollar denominated term loan and an Islamic Murabahah facility. Total contracted financing for its Malaysian portfolio now stands at approximately $7.2 billion, with 1GW of IT power already earning revenue under long term customer agreements and 2.3GW in signed bookings.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

China Now Operates 24GW of Data Center Capacity With a Further 50GW in the Pipeline, as Inner Mongolia Emerges as the Primary Construction Hub With 89 Sites and Around 15GW of Committed Capacity

A CNN analysis published on 13 October 2026, drawing on SemiAnalysis data, found that China operates 24GW of data center capacity with a further 50GW under construction or announced, with Inner Mongolia's Ulanqab hosting 89 sites and roughly 15GW of committed capacity from operators including Alibaba and Huawei. US and Chinese export restrictions on Nvidia chips remain the principal bottleneck, but cheap land and rapidly expanding wind and solar power are accelerating construction timelines.

Source: CNN  ·  13 October 2026
 New Builds

Meta Named as the Developer Behind a 3,000 Acre, $10 Billion Data Center Campus in Mason County, Kentucky, Securing a 40 Year Property Tax Break and Committing $115 Million to Local Road and Water Infrastructure

Meta was identified at a Mason County Fiscal Court meeting on 13 October 2026 as the company behind a roughly 3,000 acre campus in northern Kentucky, with a stated investment of $10 billion per phase and potential to reach up to 2.2GW of power capacity. A community benefits agreement approved by county commissioners includes $115 million for road upgrades, a new water treatment plant and pipelines, alongside a 40 year local property tax exemption under an Industrial Revenue Bond arrangement.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  15 October 2026
 Policy

Oregon Governor Tina Kotek Orders Her Environmental Regulator to Pause Issuing Air and Water Pollution Permits for Data Centers Until July 2027, Creating a De Facto Moratorium on New and Expanded Projects

Oregon Governor Tina Kotek directed the state's environmental regulator on 9 October 2026 to stop approving air and water pollution permits for new or expanding data centers until July 2027, following an earlier pause on projects involving state owned land. A Data Center Advisory Committee is due to deliver policy recommendations by the end of 2026 to inform state legislation, with a public comment period on its preliminary report closing on 24 October.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  9 October 2026

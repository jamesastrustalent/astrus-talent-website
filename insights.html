New Builds

HUMAIN and DataVolt Break Ground on the 100MW First Phase of a 360MW AI Data Center at Neom's Oxagon Industrial District in Saudi Arabia, With the Campus on a Trajectory Towards 1.5GW of Total Capacity Powered by Neom's Renewable Energy Assets

Saudi AI venture HUMAIN and developer DataVolt started construction in September 2026 on a 100MW first phase of a 360MW facility at Neom's Oxagon industrial district on the northwest coast of Saudi Arabia, with the initial tranche targeted to be operational in 2028. The project forms part of a larger 1.5GW campus planned for Oxagon and advances HUMAIN's role as the national vehicle for sovereign AI infrastructure, drawing on Neom's wind and solar energy supply and subsea cable connectivity to Europe and Africa to support hyperscale AI workloads.

 Market Intelligence

Iraq's Ministry of Communications Announces Plans for Five Government Data Centers Across Baghdad, Mosul and Basra to Strengthen National Digital Infrastructure and Expand the Country's Sovereign Cloud Hosting Capability

Iraq's Ministry of Communications revealed plans on 9 October 2026 for five new government data centers, with three sites in Baghdad at Al Sinak, Al Intisar and the Telecommunications Tower in Al Mamoun, alongside facilities in Mosul and near the marine landing station in Basra Governorate. The ministry has not yet disclosed the capacity, build timeline or cost of the facilities, but the announcement follows a memorandum of understanding with Huawei signed at ITEX 2026 in Baghdad, signalling a concerted effort to build out Iraq's national cloud infrastructure through partnerships with major technology suppliers.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  9 October 2026
 New Builds

Nebius Signs a Letter of Intent With Volt Infrastructure to Take Capacity at the Planned 600MW Rotterdam AI Gigafactory in the Netherlands, Becoming the First Named Offtaker at the Site With Initial Capacity Targeted for Late 2029 or Early 2030

Nebius and Volt announced on 12 October 2026 that Nebius had signed a letter of intent to lease capacity at Volt's planned AI Gigafactory in the Port of Rotterdam, a six building campus with a total IT capacity of 600MW whose first phase is estimated at €7 billion including around €4.5 billion for chips and compute equipment. The agreement is subject to definitive contracts, financing, permits and confirmation of sufficient grid capacity, and realisation of the project is not guaranteed, with the precise arrangement around computing equipment still to be finalised between the two companies.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  12 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Italy's Data Center Industry Association Projects EUR 36.9 Billion in Investment Through 2036 as National Capacity Is Expected to Rise Fivefold to 2.3 Gigawatts by 2031, Positioning the Country as One of Europe's Fastest Growing Markets

Bloomberg reported on 8 October 2026 that the Italian Datacenter Association's latest industry study forecasts approximately €36.9 billion of data center investment in Italy over the next decade, driven by AI workload growth and cloud adoption, with installed capacity projected to increase from around 460MW to up to 2.3GW by 2031. The projections reflect accelerating interest from hyperscalers and private equity in Italian sites, supported by government incentive programmes and improving grid connectivity across the country.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  8 October 2026
 New Builds

CoreWeave Enters India With a 240MW Lease at AdaniConneX's Taloja Campus in Navi Mumbai, Taking All Three 80MW Direct Liquid Cooled Buildings as Sole Tenant and Targeting First Capacity Delivery in Mid 2028 With an Option to Double to 480MW

CoreWeave and AdaniConneX announced on 7 October 2026 that CoreWeave would lease 240MW across three 80MW buildings at the Taloja campus in Navi Mumbai, becoming the sole tenant across facilities purpose built for NVIDIA Vera Rubin hardware with direct liquid cooling for GPU halls and a closed loop chilled water system. The agreement includes an option to add a further 240MW at the same site, and CoreWeave also plans to establish a local office and hire staff in India as part of its market entry.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  7 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

SoftBank Group, Grab and Petroleum Sarawak Berhad Sign a Framework Agreement to Explore an AI and Digital Infrastructure Platform in Sarawak, Malaysia, With Petros to Supply Energy and SoftBank to Lead on AI Infrastructure and Capital Raising

SoftBank Group, Grab and state owned energy company Petroleum Sarawak Berhad signed a framework agreement on 8 October 2026 to explore an AI and digital infrastructure platform in Sarawak, with Petros providing energy and supporting infrastructure, SoftBank contributing AI and semiconductor expertise alongside capital raising capability, and Grab adding its regional customer platforms and applied AI capabilities. The three partners have not disclosed a site, investment figure or construction timeline, and the agreement commits them to a phased roadmap covering AI computing, robotics and local talent development.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  8 October 2026
 New Builds

AWS Files Plans for a 36 Building, 1,100 Acre Data Center Campus at the Homer City Energy Campus in Indiana County, Pennsylvania, Targeting Construction From December 2026 and Drawing Power From a New 4.5GW Natural Gas Plant on the Former Coal Station Site

Amazon Web Services filed a preliminary land development plan on 6 October 2026 for a 36 building campus across six sites in Center and Blacklick Townships, Indiana County, with each building spanning approximately 225,000 square feet across two storeys and the campus projected to employ 1,500 to 2,000 people at salaries averaging $80,000 to $100,000. The development draws power from a 4.5GW gas plant being built on the former Homer City coal station site, though local residents raised concerns about noise, water use and property boundaries at a September planning commission meeting that tabled the application.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  6 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Google Signs a 20 Year Nuclear Power Purchase Agreement With Constellation Energy for 890MW From Uprates Across Eleven Existing Plants, Part of a Broader 3.6GW Arrangement in the PJM Service Area to Secure Clean Baseload Power for Its US Data Centers

Google and Constellation Energy announced on 6 October 2026 that Google had signed a 20 year power purchase agreement covering 890MW of additional nuclear capacity to be generated through uprates at eleven of Constellation's existing units in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, adding to a separate 2.7GW supply arrangement in the PJM regional grid and bringing Google's total contracted Constellation capacity to approximately 3.6GW. The announcement followed Amazon's signing of a 20 year, 690MW nuclear PPA with Constellation the previous week tied to an uprate at the Calvert Cliffs plant in Maryland, reflecting a broader trend of hyperscalers contracting directly with nuclear generators to meet expanding US data center power requirements.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  6 October 2026

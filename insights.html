New Builds

G42 Confirms Its Abu Dhabi Stargate AI Data Center Campus Remains on Track as Bloomberg Reports Gulf States Are Advancing Five Gigawatt AI Infrastructure Plans Despite Repeated Attacks on Regional Facilities and Heightened Security Measures at Active Construction Sites

Bloomberg reported on 4 September 2026 that G42 is continuing construction on the Abu Dhabi campus earmarked for OpenAI, Oracle, Nvidia and SoftBank, with the phased 5GW programme remaining on schedule despite the physical security risks that led to an AWS outage across the UAE and Bahrain earlier in 2026. Workers at the Stargate UAE site received additional protections during the most intense period of regional conflict, and G42 confirmed the 200MW first phase targeted for delivery in 2026 had not been materially disrupted by the security environment.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  4 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Microsoft Announces Its Saudi Arabia East Cloud Region Will Open to Customers in November 2026, Enabling Saudi Organisations to Process and Store Data Within the Kingdom Under National Data Residency Requirements and Bringing Full Azure Services to Locally Hosted Infrastructure for the First Time

Microsoft announced in August 2026 that its Saudi Arabia East datacenter region will become generally available in November 2026, giving public and private sector organisations in the Kingdom access to Azure cloud services from infrastructure physically located within Saudi Arabia. The launch supports Saudi data residency obligations and builds on the company's long-standing government partnerships, complementing the broader acceleration of US cloud and AI infrastructure across the Gulf through programmes including HUMAIN and the proposed 5GW Stargate UAE campus in Abu Dhabi.

Source: Microsoft Source EMEA  ·  August 2026
 New Builds

CX Tech Files a Planning Application Valued at £16.5 Billion for a 1GW Data Center Campus at the Former Chapelcross Nuclear Site Near Annan in Scotland, Comprising Eight 120MW Buildings Across 162,000 Square Metres With a Build Programme Phased From 2027 to 2035

CX Tech Limited submitted a Planning Permission in Principle application to Dumfries and Galloway Council on 30 September 2026 for eight 120MW data center buildings totalling approximately 1GW at the decommissioned Chapelcross site, with the developer describing the campus as exclusively renewable powered and submitting a 500 page Environmental Impact Assessment alongside the application. The Scottish government indicated it will publish national guidance for large data center development before the end of 2026 and does not expect planning decisions to be made before that guidance is in place, placing the application in an extended statutory consultation ahead of any formal determination.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  30 September 2026
 Capex Watch

UK AI Cloud Provider Nscale Raises $3.36 Billion in Convertible Financing Ahead of a Planned NYSE Listing at a Target Valuation of Around $35 Billion, With Lead Investor Third Point Joined by Nvidia, Apollo, Citadel and Abu Dhabi Investment Council

Nscale announced on 25 September 2026 that it had secured $3.36 billion in convertible loan notes comprising an initial $2.36 billion tranche led by Third Point and a further $1 billion commitment from Nvidia expected in the middle of November 2026, with the notes set to convert automatically into ordinary shares on completion of its planned New York Stock Exchange listing. The company reported a contracted backlog exceeding $103 billion in total contracted value and annualised contractual revenue above $600 million, with proceeds earmarked for expanding its liquid cooled AI data centers and GPU clusters across Europe and North America.

Source: TelecomTV  ·  25 September 2026
 New Builds

Bitdeer AI Signs a Ten Year Services Agreement for a New 67MW Campus in Malaysia, Its Third Site in the Country, Purpose Built for Liquid Cooled Nvidia GB300 NVL72 Systems With Capacity Delivery Beginning in the First Quarter of 2027 and Active AI Cloud Pipeline Exceeding $10 Billion

Bitdeer AI announced on 8 October 2026 that it had signed a ten year data center services agreement for A901, a new 67MW campus in Malaysia that will sit alongside existing facilities in Cyberjaya and Johor and is purpose built for liquid cooled Nvidia GB300 NVL72 hardware. The agreement brings the company's secured AI cloud capacity to approximately 273.5MW across Malaysia, Norway and the United States, representing around 78 percent of its stated target of up to 350MW by the first quarter of 2028, with its active AI cloud pipeline now estimated to exceed $10 billion.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  8 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Thailand Moves to Finalise Nationwide Data Center Standards by the Middle of October 2026 That Would Classify Facilities Above 100MW as Hyperscale Operations Required to Locate in Industrial Estates, Following the Suspension of Work at 49 Active Sites Across the Country

Thai authorities announced that new national data center regulations were expected to be finalised by the middle of October 2026, introducing requirements covering zoning, electrical systems, building safety, backup fuel storage and environmental protection, with facilities consuming more than 100MW of power likely to be classified as hyperscale industrial operations required to locate within designated industrial zones. The new framework followed an order suspending construction at approximately 49 active sites and freezing approvals for 117 further planned facilities across Thailand while the rules were drafted, with existing operators expected to receive a transition period to bring their premises into compliance.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  September 2026
 New Builds

Crusoe Files Plans for a $4.8 Billion Data Center Campus in Jayton, West Texas, Comprising Two Buildings Totalling 1.52 Million Square Feet Listed in State Filings as Satellite Facilities of the Larger Project Hyper Programme at Childress With Construction Starting January 2027

Data Center Dynamics reported in early October 2026 that Crusoe had filed construction documents with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation for a $4.8 billion campus in Jayton, Kent County, comprising two buildings of approximately 759,000 square feet each along Country Road 233 and P2390 with construction targeted to start in January 2027 and the facilities expected online in May and July 2029 respectively. The Jayton buildings are listed in the state filings as spur buildings of Project Hyper, which also encompasses three facilities at Childress estimated at approximately $2.4 billion each, pointing to a combined west Texas AI infrastructure programme that could reach approximately $12 billion in total investment.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Virginia Governor Abigail Spanberger Signs an Executive Order Barring State Agencies From Entering Into NDAs on Data Center Projects of 25MW or More and Requiring Local Government Approval for Large Facilities, Marking a Significant Policy Shift in the World's Largest Data Center Market

Governor Spanberger signed Executive Order 22 on 18 September 2026, establishing the Data Center Accountability Framework and prohibiting executive branch agencies from entering into non disclosure agreements for data center projects, while directing that facilities above 25MW move away from by right approval to a process requiring local government consent. The governor announced plans to work with the General Assembly in the 2027 legislative session to codify the framework into permanent law, and the order also created an AI task force and instructed officials to accelerate development of noise regulations and conduct a review of diesel backup generation at data center sites across the Commonwealth.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  18 September 2026

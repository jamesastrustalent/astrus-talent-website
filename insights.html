New Builds

Microsoft Opens Its Saudi Arabia East Cloud Region This Month, Delivering Three Availability Zones Across the Eastern Province to Serve Government and Enterprise Organisations Under Vision 2030

Microsoft's Saudi Arabia East datacenter region has become available to customers this month, providing three availability zones with independent power, cooling and networking as part of its $3.2 billion commitment to Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 digital strategy. A Microsoft Innovation Hub has opened alongside the region to support organisations turning AI and cloud ambitions into practical deployment.

Source: Microsoft News  ·  November 2026

EQT Enters Exploratory Talks With UAE and Saudi Sovereign Investors Over Joint AI and Data Center Deals Using EdgeConneX as Its Primary Gulf Deployment Platform

Swedish private equity firm EQT is in discussions with sovereign wealth funds in the UAE and Saudi Arabia about joint investments in AI and data center infrastructure, with EdgeConneX as the proposed deployment platform targeting more than 10 gigawatts of new global capacity. The Gulf conversations sit within a $120 billion envelope of AI infrastructure commitments announced by the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar, with Stargate UAE and Saudi Arabia's Project Transcendence as the flagship programmes.

Source: The National  ·  3 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Norway Halts New Data Center Grid Connections Until at Least August 2027 and Plans a Licensing Regime to Prioritise Domestic AI Needs Over Large Global Cloud Operators

Grid operator Statnett stopped reserving new power connections for data centers in Norway on 9 November 2026 and the government announced plans for a mandatory licensing regime from August 2027, with crypto operations and large global cloud providers expected to face the greatest restrictions. The move comes as OpenAI is planning a facility of up to 290 megawatts in Narvik and data centers consumed approximately 2.79 terawatt hours nationally in 2025, making this one of the most significant regulatory interventions in Northern European data center development this year.

Source: TechRadar  ·  9 November 2026
 New Builds

Poste Italiane Launches a 600 Million Euro Joint Investment Fund for Data Center Projects in Italy as It Accelerates Into AI and Cloud Infrastructure Following Its Acquisition of TIM

Poste Italiane has established a 600 million euro joint investment fund to support data center projects across Italy, with an initial pilot covering 20 megawatts of new capacity, as the state controlled postal group expands into digital infrastructure following its acquisition of Telecom Italia. The fund complements a separately announced 70 megawatt facility in Lombardy, which the company intends to operate alongside Italian financial and technology partners and to use for training AI models.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Batam Residents Protest Outside Nongsa Digital Park After Months Without Reliable Piped Water as Indonesia's Data Center Boom Puts Pressure on the Island's Limited Infrastructure

Residents on Batam Island in Indonesia blocked access to Nongsa Digital Park, a cluster of at least 16 data center projects within a special economic zone, in November 2026 after some neighbourhoods had been without reliable piped water supply for several months. Local officials attributed the shortage to drought and an electrical fault rather than data center consumption, but the government has ordered operators to construct desalination plants as tensions between rapid AI infrastructure expansion and local communities grow across Southeast Asia.

Source: The Japan Times  ·  8 November 2026
 New Builds

Google Commits $15 Billion to Malaysian Data Center Campuses in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan Over 2027 and 2028, Supporting More Than One Gigawatt of Clean Energy and an 800,000 Person Skills Programme

Google announced on 4 November 2026 that it will invest $15 billion in Malaysia over the next two years, funding new data center campuses in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan alongside more than one gigawatt of clean energy capacity and a digital skills programme targeting approximately 800,000 Malaysians. The commitment builds on its earlier $2 billion investment and coincides with a separate $1 billion joint development agreement from SoftBank, Grab and PETROS in Sarawak, cementing Malaysia as a primary Southeast Asian destination for hyperscale AI infrastructure.

Source: Google Cloud Press Corner  ·  4 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

About 40 Per Cent of Planned US Data Center Capacity Is in States Whose Newly Elected Governors Have Pledged to Pause or Stop New Construction After the Midterm Elections

Analysis by Cleanview cited in a Bloomberg report published on 6 November 2026 found that governors elected in Ohio, Georgia, Michigan, New Mexico, Texas, New York and Oregon are seeking to pause or restrict data center development in states accounting for roughly 158 gigawatts of planned capacity, or about 41 per cent of the national pipeline. Bloomberg Intelligence estimates as many as 10 gigawatts of capacity originally planned for 2027 may now be delayed as a result of the regulatory shift following the midterm elections.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  6 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Riot Platforms Signs a $28 Billion 15 Year Lease With Anthropic for Its Entire 910 Megawatt Corsicana Campus in Texas as Bitcoin Mining Operators Pivot to Frontier AI Infrastructure

Riot Platforms signed a 15 year lease covering its entire 910 megawatt Corsicana campus in Texas with Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI assistant, in a deal worth approximately $28 billion that became binding on 4 November 2026. Combined with an earlier agreement at its Rockdale facility, Riot's total contracted AI data center capacity is now 1,151 megawatts representing approximately $38 billion of contracted revenue, illustrating the rapid repositioning of digital infrastructure operators towards frontier AI workloads.

Source: Baxtel  ·  10 November 2026

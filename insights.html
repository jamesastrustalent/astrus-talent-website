New Builds

Humain Issues a $1 Billion Construction Tender for a 100 MW AI Data Center in Al Saad, East of Riyadh, With Bids Due by 8 November and Phase One Targeted for October 2028

Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund backed Humain has invited contractors to bid for an estimated $1 billion contract to build a 100 MW AI data center on a 350,200 square metre site in Al Saad, east of Riyadh, with an option to expand to 200 MW, as MEED reported and Utilities Middle East covered on 19 October 2026. Bids close on 8 November, construction is expected to begin in January 2027, and the first phase is targeted for completion by October 2028.

Source: Utilities Middle East  ·  19 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Nebius Signs as Anchor Customer for Up to 50 MW at NEOM's Oxagon Data Center in Saudi Arabia, in a Preliminary Framework Agreement With HUMAIN and DataVolt Signed on 21 October 2026

HUMAIN and DataVolt signed a preliminary framework with Dutch AI cloud company Nebius on 21 October 2026 to provide it with up to 50 MW of capacity at the Oxagon data center at NEOM, representing half of an initial 100 MW campus already under construction and part of a wider project planned to reach 360 MW. The agreement was signed at Nvidia's GTC event in Berlin, with first capacity expected in Q4 2027.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  22 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

The UK Government Rejects the Drax Power Station Site's Bid for AI Growth Zone Status Over Insufficient Energy Delivery Plans, Prompting Drax to Scale Back to a 100 MW Grid Connected Campus

The UK government turned down a joint bid by North Yorkshire Council, the University of York, and Drax to designate the Selby biomass power station as an AI Growth Zone, having concluded the proposal lacked credible plans for energy delivery, environmental considerations and local economic benefit, as Data Center Dynamics reported on 16 October 2026. Drax has since pivoted to a smaller 100 MW data center campus that would draw power from the national grid rather than from the power station itself.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  16 October 2026
 New Builds

French AI Infrastructure Company Sesterce Plans More Than €10 Billion to Convert a Former Paper Mill in Jämsä, Finland, Into a 600 MW AI Data Center Campus, With an Unnamed Leading AI Laboratory as Anchor Tenant

Sesterce announced on 8 October 2026 that it will invest more than €10 billion to redevelop the former Kaipola paper mill in Jämsä, Central Finland, into an AI campus starting at 200 MW and rising to 600 MW in a second phase, as The Next Web reported. Site clearance is already under way, construction is planned to begin before year end, and an anchor customer described only as one of the world's two leading AI laboratories has been secured.

Source: The Next Web  ·  8 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Singapore Based DayOne Raises US$3.67 Billion From a Seven Bank Syndicate for Its Johor Data Center Expansion in Malaysia, Bringing Its Malaysian Debt Platform to Approximately US$7.3 Billion

DayOne, the Singapore based hyperscale data center developer, secured US$3.67 billion in multicurrency financing from CIMB, Crédit Agricole CIB, DBS, Maybank, OCBC, Standard Chartered and UOB to fund its Johor, Malaysia, campus expansion, the company announced on 14 October 2026. The latest tranche joins an earlier June 2025 facility of the same size and takes DayOne's Malaysian debt platform to roughly US$7.3 billion, as the company progresses towards a Nasdaq listing with 962 MW of capacity in service as of September.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  14 October 2026
 New Builds

KT Cloud Breaks Ground on a 50 MW AI Data Center in Ansan, South Korea, as the First Step Towards a Target of 1 GW of AI Data Center Capacity Across the Country by 2031

South Korean telecoms and cloud group KT Cloud broke ground on a 50 MW AI data center in Ansan, Gyeonggi Province, on 22 October 2026, with construction due to complete in approximately 38 months, as Data Center Dynamics reported. The facility is designed to support liquid cooling for high performance GPU workloads throughout, and is the first in a series of investments the company expects to bring its total AI data center capacity to 1 GW nationally by 2031.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  22 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Oregon Governor Tina Kotek Directs the State Environmental Regulator to Pause Air and Water Permits for New and Expanded Data Centers Until July 2027, Introducing a De Facto Statewide Moratorium

Oregon Governor Tina Kotek wrote to the Oregon Department of Environmental Quality on 9 October 2026, directing it to pause processing air, water and wastewater permits for new or expanded data centers until 1 July 2027, creating what Data Center Dynamics described as a de facto moratorium. The order follows a September directive pausing state land requests for data centers and is intended to create space for policy and regulatory reforms ahead of the 2027 legislative session.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Meta Named as the Developer Behind a Roughly 3,000 Acre, $10 Billion Data Center Campus in Mason County, Kentucky, After County Commissioners Approve a Community Benefits Agreement

Meta was publicly identified as the company behind a roughly 3,000 acre data center campus in Mason County, Kentucky, during an October 13 county commissioners meeting at which a community benefits agreement was passed. The project covers approximately 2,200 acres in its initial phase, represents a $10 billion investment commitment from Meta with potential for further expansion, and was reported by Data Center Dynamics on 15 October 2026.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  15 October 2026

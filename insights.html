Market Intelligence

S&P Global Forecasts Middle East Data Center Capacity to Triple by 2030, With the UAE and Saudi Arabia Set to Add Two to Three Gigawatts of New IT Power and Collectively Account for More Than 80 Per Cent of Regional Market Growth

An S&P Global analysis published on 5 October 2026 found that third party data center capacity across the Middle East is on course to triple by 2030, propelled by the Stargate UAE campus targeting 5GW in Abu Dhabi and Saudi state backed AI company Humain's programme to reach 1.9GW in the Kingdom by the same date. The agency noted that build costs run at approximately $11 million per megawatt in the UAE and $13 million per megawatt in Saudi Arabia, broadly comparable with European and North American construction costs.

Source: Reuters  ·  5 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Qatar State Data Center Provider MEEZA Signs Its Largest Contract to Date, a Long Term Lease Worth More Than QAR 1 Billion for 8MW of Capacity at a New Campus Designed to Handle Cloud and AI Workloads for One of the World's Fastest Growing Cloud Providers

MEEZA announced on 7 September 2026 that it had entered into a long term leasing agreement with an unnamed global cloud provider valued at more than QAR 1 billion ($275 million), marking the largest contract in the company's history and tripling its previously committed capacity target. The 8MW of capacity will be delivered from a purpose built data center campus designed to accommodate cloud and AI workloads, reinforcing Qatar's growing role as a sovereign digital infrastructure hub within the Gulf.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  7 September 2026
 New Builds

atNorth Announces a 75MW First Phase of a New 230MW Data Center Campus in Salo, Southwest Finland, Backed by a €2 Billion Total Investment Programme and Planned to Supply Surplus Waste Heat to the Local District Heating Network

atNorth announced on 5 October 2026 that it would develop the FIN05 campus on a 28.6 hectare site in Salo, approximately 115km west of Helsinki, with 75MW of IT capacity targeted in the first phase and potential growth to 230MW. The Nordic operator, which already runs four data centers in Finland, intends to enter long term power purchase agreements with local energy suppliers and supply surplus waste heat to Salo's district heating network, contributing to a €2 billion total programme of investment.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  5 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

AWS Secures Planning Permission From Buckinghamshire Council for Its Data Center in Iver, Clearing the Path for a Two Storey 30,500 Square Metre Facility With 27 Backup Generators to Replace 17 Industrial Units on a Site Acquired for £132.5 Million in 2022

Buckinghamshire Council granted planning consent on 1 October 2026 for Amazon Web Services to build a two storey data center at the Ridgeway Distribution Centre in Iver, replacing 17 existing industrial and warehouse units with a single 30,525 sq m building and a 27 generator backup power compound. As conditions of consent, Amazon agreed to contribute £600,382 toward local air quality mitigation projects and £73,000 for improvements to public rights of way near the site.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Japan's Largest Power Generator JERA Signs a Memorandum of Understanding With Dell Technologies and RHAELM Holdings for a $15 Billion, 400MW AI Data Center at Chiba Thermal Power Station, Bypassing Grid Queues Through a Behind the Meter Power Arrangement

JERA, Dell Technologies, and RHAELM Holdings announced a non binding memorandum of understanding on 1 October 2026 to co develop a $15 billion AI data center at JERA's Chiba Thermal Power Station, targeting 400MW of IT capacity to come online in phases from 2028 with full capacity by 2029. The behind the meter structure draws power directly from JERA's generation asset rather than competing for grid interconnection, with Dell providing standardised compute through its AI Factory and RHAELM leading project delivery and financing.

Source: Nippon.com  ·  1 October 2026
 New Builds

MC Digital Realty Begins Construction of KIX15, a 24MW Data Center at Its Osaka Campus in Ibaraki City, Japan, as the Fifth Building on the KIX Site and Expected to Reach Operational Status by the End of 2028

MC Digital Realty, the joint venture between Digital Realty and Mitsubishi Corporation, commenced construction of KIX15 on 7 October 2026, a 24MW data center at its Osaka campus in Ibaraki City, Osaka Prefecture, which will be the fifth building on the KIX site and is scheduled to be operational by the end of 2028. The KIX campus has expanded steadily since KIX10 opened in 2017 and now offers more than 70MW of total capacity, with KIX15 further strengthening Osaka's standing as one of Japan's primary data center markets alongside Tokyo.

Source: GlobeNewswire  ·  7 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Google Signs Two Long Term Power Agreements With Constellation Energy Totalling 3.6GW in the PJM Service Area, Including a 20 Year Nuclear PPA for 890MW That Will Fund a $4.3 Billion Investment Programme Across 11 Nuclear Generating Units in Illinois, New Jersey and Pennsylvania

Google announced on 6 October 2026 that it had entered into two long term energy agreements with Constellation Energy totalling 3.6GW in the PJM service area, comprising a 20 year nuclear power purchase agreement for 890MW and a 15 year deal for 2.7GW of broader regional generating capacity. Constellation said the nuclear contract would support a $4.3 billion investment programme across 11 nuclear generating units, securing 4,400 existing jobs and creating 7,200 new positions during the build out.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  6 October 2026
 New Builds

AI Infrastructure Company Crusoe Files Permits for a $4.8 Billion Campus in Jayton, Texas, Comprising Two 759,000 Square Foot Data Center Buildings in Kent County as Satellite Facilities to Its Larger Project Hyper Campus in Childress, With Construction Starting in January 2027

Crusoe filed permit applications on 2 October 2026 with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation for two data center buildings in Jayton, Kent County, each spanning 759,260 sq ft and representing a total campus investment of $4.8 billion. The Jayton site is designated as a satellite of Project Hyper, a multi site AI programme under development in Childress, Texas, with construction scheduled to start in late January 2027 and the first building targeting operational status in May 2029.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2 October 2026

Capex Watch

Saudi Arabia's Humain Plans $2.5 Billion Fund to Finance 250MW of New AI Data Center Capacity

PIF-backed AI company Humain is seeking to raise an initial $2.5 billion from global and domestic investors through a dedicated fund to finance data center construction in Saudi Arabia, targeting 250 megawatts of capacity developed in partnership with Al Moammar Information Systems and with options to scale to 1 gigawatt. The raise represents the latest step in Humain's plan to build a sovereign AI infrastructure platform for the Kingdom underpinned by locally hosted and controlled compute assets.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  3 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Accenture and AWS Sign Six Year Agreement to Accelerate Cloud and AI Adoption Across the Middle East

Accenture and Amazon Web Services have signed a six year strategic collaboration agreement to help organisations across the Middle East modernise infrastructure and deploy AI at scale, with a dedicated regional team of architects and delivery leads supporting cloud migration, data and AI, and enterprise platform programmes. The agreement targets key sectors including energy, financial services and the public sector across the region.

Source: The National  ·  1 September 2026
 Capex Watch

Schwarz Group Commits €5.6 Billion to 240MW Renewable Powered AI Data Center in Northern Germany

Schwarz Group, parent company of the Lidl and Kaufland retail chains, has committed €5.6 billion to develop a 240 megawatt data center in Dummersdorf, Mecklenburg Vorpommern, designed for the group's own internal use and powered by regional offshore and onshore wind energy, with a letter of intent signed to channel waste heat back into the local district heating network. The facility is planned for delivery by 2033 with scope to scale to 1 gigawatt by 2045, and joins an existing Schwarz Digits campus in Lübbenau, Brandenburg.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  27 August 2026
 New Builds

Digital Realty Breaks Ground on 15MW AI Ready ZUR4 Data Center at Its Expanded Zurich Campus

Digital Realty has broken ground on ZUR4, a 15 megawatt AI ready data center in Glattbrugg, Zurich, adding a fourth facility to what the company describes as Switzerland's best connected campus alongside ZUR1, ZUR2 and ZUR3. The approximately 6,300 square metre building is designed for high density AI, machine learning, cloud connectivity and data sovereign workloads, is expected to be operational by 2028, and forms part of Digital Realty's European portfolio which runs entirely on renewable energy.

Source: Digital Realty via GlobeNewswire  ·  27 August 2026
 Market Intelligence

Asia Pacific Data Center Pipeline Hits Record 26.5GW in H1 2026 as AI Pre Leasing Tightens Supply

Asia Pacific's data center development pipeline reached an all time high of 26.5GW in the first half of 2026, growing by 7.1GW across the period, with South East Asia accounting for approximately half of all capacity under construction at 2.38GW, according to Cushman and Wakefield. Malaysia led the region with 1.04GW under construction, while Johor retained the top position by combined operational and future capacity at over 4GW, as aggressive AI pre leasing continued to tighten available supply.

Source: Cushman & Wakefield  ·  August 2026
 New Builds

CoreWeave Launches 360MW AI Data Center Expansion in Indonesia as Its First Asia Pacific Market

CoreWeave has announced plans for three AI data center facilities across Greater Jakarta totalling 360MW of contracted IT power, marking the company's first entry into the Asia Pacific region with facilities expected to come online in 2028. The expansion brings CoreWeave's AI cloud platform closer to enterprises, AI native companies and governments across South East Asia, where demand for locally hosted compute capacity is accelerating as data localisation requirements intensify.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 August 2026
 Market Intelligence

Kimmeridge Warns up to Half of Planned US Data Centers Face Delays or Cancellation Amid Infrastructure Constraints

Investment firm Kimmeridge Energy Management has warned that as many as half of planned data centers in the United States are at risk of delays or cancellation, citing growing political opposition and the complexity of building large scale physical infrastructure as technology capital meets real world constraints. Managing partner Ben Dell noted that US gas demand forecasts tied to new AI power plant construction may need to be revised downward if project timelines continue to slip.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  26 August 2026
 New Builds

Oracle and OpenAI Mark First Building Milestone at the $16 Billion Stargate Campus in Saline Township, Michigan

Oracle and OpenAI have confirmed that the exterior of the first of three 550,000 square foot data center buildings at the one gigawatt Stargate Michigan campus in Saline Township is nearing completion, just three months after construction began in June 2026. Developed by Related Digital with Blackstone financing, the 250 acre campus uses closed loop non evaporative cooling, will be powered entirely by DTE Energy, and is on track to deliver all three data halls through 2027.

Source: OpenAI  ·  August 2026

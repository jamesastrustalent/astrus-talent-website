New Builds

Oman and the UK Seal a £1 Billion AI Data Center Deal for Salalah as Sultan Haitham Visits Britain, With UK Developer Yamna Confirmed to Lead the First Phase of the Project

UK developer Yamna signed an agreement with Oman's Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology on 24 October 2026 to build an AI data center in Salalah, with the first phase valued at more than £1 billion, or approximately $1.3 billion. Sultan Haitham bin Tarik was on a state visit to the United Kingdom at the time of signing, and UK Export Finance has signalled plans to raise its lending capacity for British exports to Oman to as much as £7 billion to support the bilateral investment programme.

 Market Intelligence

Data Center World Launches Its First Middle East Edition in Abu Dhabi on 17 and 18 November 2026 as GCC Capacity Is Forecast to Triple by 2030, Driven by Surging AI Infrastructure Investment

Data Center World announced on 28 October 2026 that its inaugural Middle East edition would take place at the Dusit Thani hotel in Abu Dhabi, with programme coverage spanning the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar and Bahrain alongside a market outlook to 2030. Participants include UAE government ministries and established regional operators such as Khazna, Core42 and Tabreed, reflecting the Gulf's rapid emergence as one of the world's most actively invested data center markets.

Source: Zawya  ·  28 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

UK Autumn Budget Introduces a British Data Center Covenant Granting Strategically Important Sites Priority Grid Connections in Exchange for Community Investment Commitments

The UK Chancellor announced a British Data Center Covenant in the Autumn Budget delivered on 28 October 2026, requiring operators to invest in local communities, create jobs and protect water supplies. Critically important sites will become eligible for reserved and reallocated grid connection capacity and permission to install temporary on-site generation while awaiting a permanent connection, backed by new powers in the Planning and Infrastructure Act.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  28 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

European Commission Tender for Up to Seven AI Gigafactories Closes on 12 November 2026, Targeting More Than €30 Billion in Investment and at Least 100,000 Advanced AI Processors Per Site

The European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking opened its call for proposals on 30 July 2026 to select up to seven AI gigafactory sites across EU member states, with bids due by 12 November. The initiative aims to combine approximately €10 billion in public funding with at least €20 billion in private co-investment, and selected gigafactories are expected to become operational within 18 months of contract signing. A preliminary round drew 76 expressions of interest from 16 member states.

Source: European Commission  ·  30 July 2026
 New Builds

AirTrunk Closes a AU$4.25 Billion Green Loan Backed by 32 Lenders to Fund Its SYD3 Campus in Western Sydney, Targeting 400 MW Across 42 Data Halls on Completion in 2028

AirTrunk announced on 27 October 2026 that it had closed a AU$4.25 billion green loan, backed by 32 institutions including DBS, HSBC, MUFG and National Australia Bank, to finance continued development of its SYD3 campus on an 8.3 hectare site in Western Sydney. The campus is expected to reach 400 MW across 42 data halls and 70,000 square metres of technical space when it completes in 2028, bringing AirTrunk's total committed debt financing to approximately AU$38.4 billion globally.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  27 October 2026
 New Builds

CDC Data Centres Plans a 1.4 GW, A$15 Billion Data Center Campus Near Wagga Wagga in New South Wales and Offers the City a Community Benefits Package Worth Up to A$225 Million

CDC Data Centres has lodged plans for a 1.4 GW campus on a 100 hectare council owned site at Gregadoo, about 11 kilometres south of Wagga Wagga in New South Wales, which would rank among the largest data center developments in Australia's history. The four building scheme would cost an estimated A$15 billion, and on 13 October 2026 CDC offered a community benefits package worth up to A$225 million, equivalent to 1.5% of the build cost, as the council considers whether to sell the land to the developer.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  30 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Verizon Signs a $2 Billion Framework Agreement With AWS to Scale Distributed AI Infrastructure for Agentic and Generative Workloads Across the United States

Verizon announced a commercial framework agreement with AWS valued at more than $2 billion on 26 October 2026, building on an earlier AI Connect fibre deal from November 2025 in which AWS serves as Verizon's preferred public cloud partner. Management said meaningful revenue from the agreement is expected to materialise from 2027 and to grow over the following five to ten years, with the deal designed to deliver high capacity connectivity for AWS data centers carrying agentic and generative AI workloads.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  27 October 2026
 New Builds

AIB Data Centers Plans a 50 MW AI Data Center at a Former Bitcoin Mine in Spartanburg County, South Carolina, Under a 12 Year Lease Agreement With European Cloud Provider Nebius

AIB Data Centers announced on 29 October 2026 that it would convert a former cryptocurrency mining facility in Spartanburg County, South Carolina, into a 50 MW AI data center under a 12 year lease with Nebius, targeting two 25 MW data halls for delivery in the fourth quarter of 2027. The deal anticipates approximately $1.2 billion in total investment with at least $400 million in capital spending and 28 full time jobs, and benefits from an exemption in the county's recently adopted one year data center moratorium, which excludes former mining sites at or under 65 MW.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  29 October 2026

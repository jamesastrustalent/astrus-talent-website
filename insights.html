Market Intelligence

Microsoft Confirms Its Saudi Arabia East Azure Cloud Region Will Open in November 2026, Offering Three Availability Zones and Local Data Residency in the Eastern Province as Part of a $10 Billion Gulf Commitment Running Through 2030

Microsoft announced at the LEAP 2026 conference in Riyadh on 1 September that its Saudi Arabia East data center region, located in the Eastern Province, will be available to cloud customers from November 2026, nearly two years after construction was completed. The region will offer three availability zones with independent power, cooling and networking, enabling local data residency to meet Saudi regulatory requirements, and an IDC study projects it will contribute $44 billion to the Saudi economy and support roughly 100,000 jobs between 2027 and 2030.

Source: Saudi Press Agency  ·  1 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Submer and ACES Sign Memorandum of Understanding to Jointly Develop AI Ready Data Center Capacity in Saudi Arabia, Covering Facility Design, Advanced Liquid Cooling and Potential Joint Investment in Hyperscale and High Performance Computing Projects

Submer, the liquid cooling and AI infrastructure company, and ACES, a Saudi Arabia based technology services firm, signed a memorandum of understanding on 30 September to jointly pursue data center projects in the Kingdom, including advisory services, facility design and build, IT integration and advanced thermal management. The agreement targets hyperscale and high performance computing workloads and extends to potential joint investment in Saudi data center capacity aimed at AI deployment.

Source: Submer  ·  30 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Parkovyi Data Center in Kyiv Goes Permanently Offline After Sustaining Critical Damage in a Russian Bomb Attack, With Customer Data Migrated to Backup Sites in the EU and On Site Restoration Described as Impossible

Ukraine's Parkovyi Data Center in Kyiv went fully offline on 1 October 2026 after sustaining critical damage in a Russian bomb strike, with the facility's operators confirming that on site restoration is not possible and that all employees are safe. The attack followed a series of Russian drone strikes on 23 and 24 September that destroyed equipment at two Ukrainian hosting companies, disabled internet exchange points and cut connectivity for around 100,000 households across Kyiv.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 October 2026
 New Builds

AWS Receives Planning Permission From Buckinghamshire Council for a 30,500 Square Metre Data Center at the Ridgeway Distribution Centre in Iver, Including 27 Standby Generators and a £600,000 Contribution Toward Local Air Quality Mitigation

Buckinghamshire Council approved Amazon Web Services' application on 28 September to demolish 17 industrial and warehouse buildings at the Ridgeway Distribution Centre in Iver and replace them with a single two storey data center, an MV building, substation infrastructure and a generator yard housing 27 standby generators. Planning officers noted some risk of landscape harm but concluded that the project's public benefits, including employment creation and critical digital infrastructure provision, outweighed the objections.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Fire at a Data Center in the Geumcheon District of Seoul Forces the Evacuation of 105 People, With Firefighters Taking More Than Three Hours to Extinguish a Blaze Originating in a UPS Transformer Room

Firefighters were called to a data center in the Gasan area of Seoul at 11:55 on Tuesday 29 September after a fire broke out in a transformer in the facility's uninterruptible power supply room, with 80 officers and 14 fire engines dispatched and the blaze extinguished at 15:13 with no casualties among the 105 evacuees. The fire department has not formally identified the facility, though news footage appears consistent with the STT GDC Seoul 1 site, a 30 megawatt campus spanning 40,000 square metres that commenced operations in July 2026.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  29 September 2026
 Capex Watch

Australian Data Center Operator Firmus Targets an A$43.7 Billion Valuation as It Opens Bookbuild on 6 October for What Would Be the Second Largest IPO in Australian History, Backed by Nvidia, Blackstone and Coatue Management

Firmus Grid Ltd priced its initial public offering at A$11 per share on 1 October, implying a market capitalisation of approximately A$43.7 billion (US$30.3 billion), with bookbuild running from 6 to 7 October, the retail offer from 12 to 19 October and an ASX debut planned for 26 October 2026. Founded in 2019 and backed by Nvidia, Blackstone, Coatue Management and Jane Street, the company is seeking to raise at least US$5 billion and has reportedly secured compute agreements with OpenAI and Meta as anchor customers for its AI data center portfolio.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  1 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Amazon and Constellation Energy Sign a Power Purchase Agreement Spanning 20 Years to Support More Than $3 Billion in Investment at the Calvert Cliffs Nuclear Plant in Maryland, Adding 190 Megawatts of New Capacity and Extending the Facility's Operational Life

Amazon and Constellation Energy announced on 30 September a power purchase agreement spanning 20 years under which Amazon will take 690 megawatts of output from the Calvert Cliffs nuclear plant in Lusby, Maryland, enabling Constellation to add roughly 190 megawatts of new generating capacity due to enter service between 2030 and 2032 and to relicense the facility for a further 20 years. The deal also covers a retail supply arrangement for Amazon's electricity needs across the 13 state PJM regional grid, and was announced after Amazon separately withdrew plans to build a data center campus adjacent to the plant following community opposition.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  30 September 2026
 New Builds

Applied Digital Confirms Its $3.2 Billion Delta Forge 2 AI Data Center Campus Will Be Built Near Brookwood, Alabama, on 1,300 Acres in Tuscaloosa County to Serve an Undisclosed Investment Grade Hyperscaler With 210 Megawatts of AI Compute Capacity

Applied Digital announced on 25 September that its Delta Forge 2 project, first disclosed in June as a 210 megawatt data center secured for an investment grade hyperscaler, will be located on a 1,300 acre site near Brookwood in Tuscaloosa County, Alabama, at a total cost of $3.2 billion, with a campus spanning 1.2 million square feet and operations expected to begin in 2028. The developer is committing $270 million in community benefits payments over 20 years to the Tuscaloosa County Economic Development Authority, alongside approximately $131 million in projected tax revenue, and the project is expected to create up to 1,000 construction jobs and 100 permanent roles.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  25 September 2026

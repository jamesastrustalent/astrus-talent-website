New Builds

Humain Secures 211 Land Plots Across Saudi Arabia to Support 6.6GW Data Center Expansion Target

State backed Saudi AI company Humain has secured 211 individual plots of land distributed across the Kingdom to underpin its data center build out, targeting 6.6GW of compute capacity by 2034 with government subsidised electricity. Chief executive Tareq Amin cited geographic diversity and multiple fibre optic routes as central pillars of the strategy, which aims to position Saudi Arabia as a global AI compute hub under Vision 2030.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  August 2026
 Market Intelligence

AMD, Cisco and Humain Form Joint Venture to Deploy 1GW of AI Infrastructure Across Saudi Arabia

AMD, Cisco and state backed Saudi AI firm Humain have established a joint venture to deliver 1GW of AI compute infrastructure across the Kingdom over five years, combining AMD Instinct MI450 Series GPUs with Cisco networking across Humain operated data centers. A first phase of 100MW was scheduled for delivery in the second quarter of 2026, supporting Humain's ambition to reach approximately 6GW of national data center capacity within a decade.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2026

atNorth Acquired by Equinix and CPP Investments for $4.2 Billion in Major Nordic Data Center Deal

Partners Group has agreed to sell Nordic data center operator atNorth to a consortium of Equinix and the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board at an enterprise value of $4.2 billion, with CPP taking a 60 per cent stake and Equinix 40 per cent. atNorth will continue to operate under its own brand across Iceland, Denmark, Sweden and Finland while gaining access to Equinix's global interconnection platform.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2026
 New Builds

EdgeMode Plans 300MW AI Data Center Campus Near Toledo with On Site Hydrogen Fuel Cell Power

EdgeMode and infrastructure investor Blackberry AIF have unveiled DC Malpica, a 300MW AI data center campus in the Mora district of Toledo, Spain, backed by a €3 billion investment across two facilities totalling 300,000 square metres. The campus is designed to generate its own electricity using hydrogen fuel cell technology from Bloom Energy, with a ready to build target of early 2027 as Spain establishes itself as one of Europe's fastest growing data center markets.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2026
 Market Intelligence

Asia Data Center Debt Financing Nears $29 Billion as Banks Approach Sector Exposure Limits

Data center debt financing across Asia has neared $29 billion, pushing banks toward their sector exposure limits and prompting lenders to become more selective about which projects to support, according to Barclays. The surge in lending reflects rapid infrastructure investment across South and South East Asia, with banks expected to favour established borrowers and higher yielding transactions as demand for AI compute capacity continues to outpace available capital.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  31 August 2026
 New Builds

Hyundai Commits $4 Billion AI Data Center to South Korea Hub as Part of $6.3 Billion Clean Energy Complex

South Korean automaker Hyundai has signed a memorandum of understanding with the South Korean government and Jeonbuk State to build a $6.3 billion integrated innovation hub in Saemangeum, Gunsan City, anchored by a $4 billion AI data center planned to house up to 50,000 GPUs in phases. The complex also encompasses a 200MW hydrogen electrolyser and gigawatt scale solar generation, reflecting the convergence of AI compute and clean energy investment across South Korea's industrial base.

Source: Data Center Knowledge  ·  2026
 Capex Watch

SoftBank AI Data Center Firm SB Energy Files for US IPO to Fund Texas and Ohio Campus Build Out

SB Energy, a data center and clean power company backed by SoftBank Group, has filed for a US initial public offering to accelerate construction of AI compute campuses in Texas and Ohio, where it has 8.8GW of capacity contracted or under construction. Key projects include a 1.2GW facility serving OpenAI in Milam County, Texas, and the PORTS Pike Technology Campus at the former Portsmouth Gaseous Diffusion Plant in Ohio, developed in partnership with the US Department of Energy.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  1 September 2026
 New Builds

AWS Files for $1.2 Billion Four Building Data Center Campus in Wharton County Near Houston, Texas

Amazon Web Services has submitted planning applications for a four building data center campus in Boling, Wharton County, near Houston, representing a combined investment of approximately $1.2 billion across buildings each of around 189,000 square feet. Construction commenced in August 2026, with the first three facilities scheduled for completion in early 2027 and the fourth in August 2027, as AWS pursues an accelerated capacity expansion programme under a broader $200 billion capital plan for the year.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  August 2026

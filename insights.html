Market Intelligence

Iran's Security Chief Warns Gulf Data Centres and AI Facilities Could Be Attacked if War Resumes, Following Drone Strikes in March That Briefly Disabled Amazon Web Services Sites in the UAE

Al Jazeera reported on 13 October 2026 that Mohsen Rezaei explicitly named cables, data centres and AI campuses across the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Bahrain as potential targets. Iran's parliament speaker separately urged states and investors to withhold land, energy and infrastructure from AI firms linked to military operations, raising fresh questions about supply security across the Gulf's rapidly expanding data center market.

Source: Al Jazeera  ·  13 October 2026
 New Builds

Humain Issues an Estimated $1 Billion Request for Proposals for a 100 MW AI Data Center in Riyadh, Designed for Rack Densities of Up to 250 kW to Support High Density GPU Clusters, With Bids Due 8 November

Saudi Arabia's state owned AI company launched the engineering, procurement and construction tender on 18 October 2026 for a site in Riyadh's Al Saad area, with phase one completion targeted for October 2028. An optional second phase would bring the total capacity of the facility to 200 MW, continuing Humain's rapid procurement programme as the kingdom scales its AI infrastructure base.

Source: Utilities Middle East  ·  18 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

UK Government Rejects North Yorkshire's AI Growth Zone Bid to Power a 1.2 GW Data Center With the Drax Biomass Plant in Selby, Citing Insufficient Evidence of Credible Energy Delivery and Environmental Planning

Reported by Data Center Dynamics on 16 October 2026, the decision removes AI Growth Zone designation from the proposal but does not prevent Drax from pursuing a separate 100 MW facility at the site drawing power from the national grid. Critics noted that Drax emitted 14.1 million tonnes of CO2 equivalent in 2025, undermining its claim to operate a carbon negative data center at the location.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  16 October 2026
 New Builds

CX Tech Files Plans for a £16.5 Billion, 1 GW Data Center Campus Across Eight Buildings at the Former Chapelcross Nuclear Site in Scotland, With Construction Phases Targeted Between 2027 and 2035

The application in principle was validated by Dumfries and Galloway Council on 28 September 2026 for a 55 hectare site near Annan that sits beside a 132 kV grid supply point, as reported by Data Center Dynamics in October 2026. CX Tech says the campus would run entirely on renewables and use closed loop cooling with no evaporation, with the developer projecting up to 750 construction jobs and approximately 450 permanent roles if consent is granted.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  October 2026
 Market Intelligence

More Than Three Quarters of Large Data Centers Planned or Under Construction Across Sydney and Melbourne Cannot Confirm a Grid Connection, as Australia's $70 Billion Data Center Ambition Faces Grid Strain and Growing Community Opposition

A Bloomberg review published on 22 October 2026 found that only 23 per cent of 40 or more facilities in Australia's two largest cities had publicly confirmed power agreements, with stretched electricity networks and local opposition now affecting project timelines. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced that large scale operators would be required to become net generators of power, with legislation expected early in 2027, as protests linked to energy and water use have spread to Japan, Malaysia, Indonesia and India.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  22 October 2026
 Capex Watch

AirTrunk Plans an Additional $1 Billion Investment in Its Inzai Campus in Japan to Add Liquid Cooling for High Density AI Workloads, Bringing Its Total Japan Commitment to Approximately $9 Billion

The expansion of the TOK1 facility in Chiba Prefecture, reported by Data Center Dynamics in October 2026, will be funded by a green loan led by Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, MUFG Bank and United Overseas Bank. AirTrunk's chief executive said TOK1 is likely to be the first campus in Japan capable of hosting large scale AI deployments, with the company targeting a total Japan portfolio of more than 1 GW.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Nearly Half of US Data Centers Planned for 2026 Have Been Delayed or Cancelled, as Supply Chain Bottlenecks and Grid Constraints Replace Chip Availability as the Principal Barriers to Development

A Stacker analysis published on 19 October 2026 found that of the approximately 12 GW of US data center capacity targeted for the year, only around one third was actively under construction, with lead times for high voltage transformers stretching from 12 to 18 months before 2020 to three to five years today. The findings align with separate estimates from TD Cowen and Kimmeridge, both of which put roughly 45 per cent of planned US capacity at risk of delay or cancellation.

Source: Stacker via ABC News  ·  19 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Amazon Withdraws Its Application for an 800,000 Square Foot Data Center Campus at the Former George Washington University Site in Ashburn, Virginia, After Community Opposition, and Offers 25 Acres for a Community Arts Centre and Sports Facility

Loudoun County Board Chair Phyllis Randall announced the reversal on 20 October 2026, resolving a dispute over the 122 acre site that Amazon purchased for $427 million, with the remainder earmarked for mixed use development with housing as the principal component. The withdrawal is a significant setback for data center expansion in one of the world's most concentrated data center corridors, and follows a separate proposal from a Virginia senator for a three year statewide moratorium on new approvals.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  20 October 2026

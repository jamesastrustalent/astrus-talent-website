Capex Watch

Humain Secures 211 Land Plots Across Saudi Arabia in Record Land Drive

State backed AI venture Humain has secured 211 plots of land across Saudi Arabia through a coordinated effort involving 16 government entities, unlocking access to more than 14GW of potential capacity. The programme advances Saudi Arabia's goal of building approximately 6GW of data center capacity over the coming decade.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  August 2026
 Capex Watch

Asprofin Bank Proposes $12 Billion Sovereign Data Center Programme Across Three UAE Sites

Asprofin Bank Corporation has unveiled plans for a large scale sovereign data center programme spanning three strategic UAE sites including Hatta, Jebel Ali and MBR Solar Park, with a potential investment envelope of up to $12 billion. The phased programme aims to deliver between 750MW and 1,280MW of sovereign AI computing capacity over a 10 to 15 year timeline.

Source: Yahoo Finance  ·  30 May 2026
 Capex Watch

SoftBank Pledges €75 Billion to Build 5GW of AI Data Centers in France

SoftBank Group has committed up to €75 billion to develop and operate 5GW of AI data center capacity in northern France, the single largest investment announced at the 2026 Choose France summit. The first phase will deliver 3.1GW across the Hauts-de-France region, powered by low carbon electricity supplied by state utility EDF.

Source: SoftBank Group  ·  31 May 2026
 Market Intelligence

Europe's Data Center Market Set for 20% Capacity Growth in 2026, Says CBRE

Europe's total data center market is on course to reach 13GW of capacity by year end, representing growth of approximately 20%, according to new CBRE research. The hyperscaler self-build segment is forecast to expand by 22% to 4.3GW, marking the seventeenth consecutive year of double digit growth in that category.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2026
 Market Intelligence

Asia Pacific Data Center Pipeline Hits Record 26.5GW as AI Demand Accelerates

Asia Pacific's data center development pipeline reached a record 26.5GW in H1 2026, adding 7.1GW in just six months according to Cushman and Wakefield's latest report. Johor leads the region with over 4GW of combined operational and planned capacity, while tight vacancy rates in Tokyo and Singapore signal continued strong demand.

Source: Cushman & Wakefield  ·  August 2026
 New Platform

DigitalBridge and JEXI Launch Nippon Gateway Infrastructure as New Japan Data Center Platform

DigitalBridge and Japan Extensive Infrastructure have formed Nippon Gateway Infrastructure, a new colocation platform seeded with NEC Corporation's data center assets across Greater Tokyo and Greater Osaka. The platform plans to grow through acquisitions, facility expansions and greenfield development to meet Japan's rising AI and enterprise demand.

Source: BusinessWire  ·  1 July 2026
 New Builds

AWS Files for $1.2 Billion Four-Building Data Center Campus Outside Houston

Amazon Web Services has filed plans for a $1.2 billion data center campus in Boling, Wharton County, spanning approximately 2,700 acres southwest of Houston. Construction of the four-building Project Eagle campus commenced in August 2026, with the facility set to deliver more than 75MW of critical capacity as part of AWS's broader $4 billion Texas investment programme.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  August 2026
 New Builds

OpenAI Announces $20 Billion Project Camellia Data Center Campus in Georgia

OpenAI has unveiled Project Camellia, a $20 billion AI data center campus spanning roughly 1,400 acres in Effingham County, Georgia, near Savannah. The facility will draw 3.2GW of power from Georgia Power in phases between 2028 and 2032, creating approximately 1,000 permanent jobs and contributing $80 million to a community fund.

Source: OpenAI  ·  July 2026

Market Intelligence

Iran's Security Chief Warns Gulf Data Centers and AI Facilities Face Attack Risk if Conflict With the United States Resumes, Following IRGC Strikes on AWS Sites in the UAE and Bahrain and Published Target Lists Naming Major US Technology Firms

Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei warned on 13 October 2026 that cables, data centers and AI facilities across the Gulf could be targeted if hostilities with the United States were to resume, pointing to the region's vulnerability through undersea internet cables. The warning follows Iranian drone strikes on AWS facilities in the UAE and Bahrain earlier in 2026 and an IRGC published list of 18 US technology companies it deemed legitimate targets, naming Google, Microsoft, Oracle, Nvidia and G42 amongst them.

Source: Al Jazeera  ·  13 October 2026
 Capex Watch

S&P Global Finds the UAE and Saudi Arabia Will Drive Over 80% of Gulf Data Center Expansion Through 2030, Adding Up to 3 GW of IT Power as Humain Targets 1.9 GW in Saudi Arabia and the Stargate UAE Campus Ramps in Abu Dhabi

An S&P Global Ratings report published in early October 2026 forecasts the two countries will nearly triple regional capacity, with Humain's Saudi programme targeting 1.9 GW by 2030 and scaling to as much as 6.6 GW by 2034. The UAE is accelerating faster, with the initial 1 GW phase of the Stargate UAE campus in Abu Dhabi expected to be completed within three years, whilst Knight Frank separately forecasts Middle East capacity reaching 3.2 GW by 2027.

Source: Khaleej Times  ·  5 October 2026
 Capex Watch

SWI Group Raises €800 Million at a €6.1 Billion Valuation Through a New Share Issue on Euronext Amsterdam, Bringing Total Equity Since March 2026 to Over €1 Billion to Fund AI Data Center Expansion Across Europe and the United States

Announced on 12 October 2026, the raise will fund power infrastructure, compute deployments and a transatlantic development pipeline. SWI is repurposing 15 former Bitcoin mining sites totalling 1.3 GW acquired through Genesis Digital Assets in the US and Sweden for AI workloads, whilst divesting the acquired company's sites in Kazakhstan.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  12 October 2026
 New Builds

VivoPower Mobilises Construction on a 40 MW First Phase AI Data Center at Its Mo i Rana Site in Northern Norway, Powered Entirely by Renewable Hydroelectricity, With a Preferred AI Tenant Selected but Not Yet Named

Announced on 12 October 2026, the project converts an operational 41.5 MW industrial facility at Mo i Rana, with local contractors Øijord & Aanes engaged for site works and a full mechanical and electrical fit out tender due to be awarded shortly. VivoPower selected its preferred AI tenant following a competitive process in June 2026, and a further 40 MW of expansion capacity is available for future phases at the same site.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026
 New Builds

CoreWeave Enters India With 240 MW of AI Data Center Capacity at AdaniConneX's Taloja Campus in Navi Mumbai, Taking Sole Tenancy Across Three 80 MW Buildings Designed Around Direct Liquid Cooling and Nvidia Vera Rubin Infrastructure

Announced on 7 October 2026, the deal includes an option for a further 240 MW at the same campus, taking potential capacity to 480 MW. The Taloja facility is designed for high density AI workloads using non evaporative chilled water cooling, with the first phase expected to be available in mid-2028 as CoreWeave establishes its second Asian market.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  7 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Asia Pacific's Data Center Development Pipeline Reaches a Record 26.5 GW in the First Half of 2026 as Advanced AI Leasing Tightens Supply, With Southeast Asia Accounting for Roughly Half of All Capacity Under Construction Across the Region

Cushman & Wakefield's APAC Data Centre H1 2026 Update shows the pipeline grew by 7.1 GW in the first half of the year, encompassing 4.8 GW under construction and 21.7 GW in planning. Colocation vacancy fell from 10.9% to 10.3% as hyperscalers and AI platforms accelerated advance leasing activity, with Southeast Asia holding the largest share of under construction capacity in the region.

Source: Data Center Knowledge  ·  October 2026
 Market Intelligence

US Data Center Investment Surpasses Residential Housing Spending for the First Time, With Q2 2026 Bureau of Economic Analysis Data Placing Information Processing Equipment Expenditure at $752 Billion Against Housing's $748 Billion

Analysis published by Morgan Lewis on 7 October 2026 shows information processing investment rose 51% from its early 2021 level whilst residential investment declined 18% over the same period. Data center construction spending reached an annualised rate of approximately $75.2 billion in July 2026, around 57% above the prior year, whilst overall US construction spending contracted 3.8% year on year.

Source: Morgan Lewis  ·  7 October 2026
 Policy

Data Center Backlash Set to Shape November Midterms as Ballot Measures Across More Than Three Dozen US Localities Seek Construction Restrictions and Candidates Who Previously Championed the Industry Publicly Reverse Course

October 2026 reporting shows voters in at least 36 cities, counties and townships will weigh ballot measures in November that could ban or restrict new data center construction, following moratorium bills introduced in 11 states in 2026. Candidates across both parties who once promoted data centers as economic drivers have reversed their positions as constituents cite power grid strain, water consumption, generator noise and diminishing tax benefits as grounds for opposition.

Source: Planetizen  ·  October 2026

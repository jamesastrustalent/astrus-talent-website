New Builds

Nebius Signs on for 50 MW as Anchor Customer at NEOM's Oxagon Data Center in Saudi Arabia, Taking Half of the 100 MW First Phase of the 1.5 GW Campus

Humain and DataVolt signed a preliminary framework agreement with Nasdaq listed AI cloud company Nebius for up to 50 MW of capacity at the Oxagon data center campus in NEOM, Saudi Arabia, as reported by Data Center Dynamics on 22 October 2026. Nebius is expected to become the anchor customer, representing half of the 100 MW first phase under construction, which forms part of a wider 360 MW initial phase at the 1.5 GW campus, with phased operations expected from Q4 2027.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  22 October 2026
 New Builds

Humain Issues a $1 Billion Engineering, Procurement and Construction Tender for a 100 MW AI Data Center in Riyadh's Al-Saad District, With Bids Due by 8 November 2026 and Phase One Targeting Operations by October 2028

PIF owned Humain has issued a request for proposals for an engineering, procurement and construction contract for a 100 MW AI data center in the Al-Saad area of east Riyadh, with bids due by 8 November 2026, as reported by MEED on 16 October 2026. The estimated contract value is approximately $1 billion, with an option to expand total site capacity to 200 MW by July 2029, and the initial 100 MW is targeted as ready for service by October 2028.

Source: MEED  ·  16 October 2026
 New Builds

Era4 Unveils Plans for a Data Center Campus of Up to 1 GW at a Former Steelworks in Newport, Wales, With Up to 400,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin GPUs and First Phase Operations Due in 2027

Data center developer Era4, formerly known as Carbon3.ai, disclosed plans on 22 October 2026 for a hyperscale AI campus of up to 1 GW on a 230 acre former steel mill site in Newport, Wales, as reported by Data Center Dynamics. Newport City Council granted a change of use permission for the site in March 2026, and the first phase, branded The Mill, is expected to be operational in 2027, with the full buildout envisaged to accommodate up to 400,000 Nvidia Vera Rubin GPUs alongside community facilities including a vocational technology college.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  22 October 2026
 Regulation

Finland Orders Google to Suspend Preparatory Work at Two Data Center Sites After an Estimated 250,000 Trees Were Cleared Without Completing Required Environmental Assessments

Finland's Licensing and Supervision Authority ordered Google subsidiary Tuike Finland to halt environmentally significant preparatory work at sites in Muhos and Kajaani after tree clearing, road building and topsoil removal took place before mandatory environmental impact assessments were completed, as reported by Data Center Dynamics on 23 October 2026. Finnish police have opened an investigation, and Google acknowledged falling short of its own standards, stating it had acted on its best understanding of local planning law at the time.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  23 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

AI Data Center Boom in Australia Stalls as Only 23 Per Cent of Major Projects Have Confirmed Power Supply, Putting More Than US$70 Billion of Investment at Risk

A Bloomberg review published on 22 October 2026 found that just 23 per cent of more than 40 large data center projects under development around Sydney and Melbourne have publicly confirmed they have secured electricity supply or grid connections, with the remaining schemes representing over AU$100 billion in projected investment. The finding follows NEXTDC's dramatic earnings guidance cut the previous week, and highlights how grid strain and community opposition have become the defining constraints on the Australian market.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  22 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

SD Guthrie Says the Conflict Between the United States and Iran Is Boosting Malaysia's Appeal as a Data Center Destination as Hyperscalers Reassess Their Middle East Footprints

Malaysian landowner and agribusiness group SD Guthrie Bhd said that the ongoing conflict between the United States and Iran has increased global interest in Malaysia as a location for data center investment, as hyperscalers seek geopolitically stable alternatives to the Gulf, Bloomberg reported on 26 October 2026. The comments coincide with Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim's disclosure that SoftBank's Masayoshi Son had selected Malaysian sites for approximately $50 billion in technology investments, underlining the country's growing position in the Asia Pacific AI infrastructure race.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  26 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Meta Brings the First Phase of Its Gigawatt Prometheus Campus in New Albany, Ohio Online to Train Its Muse AI Models, Targeting Approximately One Million Nvidia GPUs at Full Buildout

Data Center Dynamics reported on 23 October 2026 that Meta has completed the first phase of its Prometheus supercluster in New Albany, Ohio, which is now in use training the company's Muse family of AI models with hundreds of thousands of Nvidia GB200 and GB300 NVL72 GPUs online. The campus is designed to eventually reach approximately one million GPUs and one gigawatt of power, with Nvidia Vera Rubin hardware due to arrive as later phases come online, making it the largest AI compute cluster in the world at full scale.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  23 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

SpaceXAI Faces a Potential $6.1 Million Fine in Mississippi After Starting Construction on Three Data Center Sites Without the Correct Stormwater Permits

The Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality issued notice to MZX Tech, a SpaceXAI affiliate, seeking civil penalties of up to $25,000 per day with a preliminary total exposure of approximately $6.1 million, after construction began at three sites near Memphis without coverage under the state's Large Construction Stormwater General Permit, as reported by Data Center Dynamics on 24 October 2026. SpaceXAI said it is cooperating with the regulator and is in discussions to resolve the matters through a single agreed order, whilst a separate federal court dispute over unpermitted gas turbines at its Colossus facility in Memphis continues.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  24 October 2026

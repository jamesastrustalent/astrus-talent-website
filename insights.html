New Builds

HUMAIN and DataVolt Begin Construction of the First AI Data Center at Neom's Oxagon Industrial City in Saudi Arabia, With 100MW of the 360MW First Phase Now Under Way and an Operational Target of 2028

State backed HUMAIN and Saudi developer DataVolt have started construction on the first AI data center at Oxagon, Neom's industrial city on the Red Sea coast, with the two companies developing 100MW of capacity as part of a 360MW first phase planned to integrate renewable energy and advanced cooling technologies for large scale AI and cloud workloads. The groundbreaking marks the start of a long term development that could see the Oxagon campus grow to a total planned capacity of 1.5GW.

Source: AGBI  ·  September 2026
 Capex Watch

Microsoft Announces Its Saudi Arabia East Datacenter Region, Comprising Three Azure Availability Zones in the Eastern Province, Will Be Available to Customers From November 2026

Microsoft confirmed that its Saudi Arabia East datacenter region, located in the Eastern Province and structured around three Azure Availability Zones, will be available to enterprise customers from November 2026, delivering local data residency, enterprise grade security, high availability and low latency access to cloud and AI services. The launch is the first Azure region on Saudi soil capable of supporting local data sovereignty requirements and forms part of Microsoft's $10 billion infrastructure commitment across the Gulf through 2030.

Source: Microsoft Source EMEA  ·  August 2026
 Market Intelligence

Eaton Agrees to Acquire Italian Medium Voltage Electrical Specialist COL Group From Oaktree for €810 Million, Adding Four Manufacturing Sites and Around 400 Employees to Its European Power Infrastructure Business

Eaton announced on 25 September that it has entered into an agreement to acquire COL Group from Oaktree's Power Opportunities strategy for an enterprise value of €810 million, with the deal expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. COL Group, which employs around 400 people across facilities in Turin, Milan, Bergamo and Catania, supplies medium voltage switchgear, grid automation systems and modular power equipment, and the acquisition is designed to expand Eaton's European production capacity to meet sustained demand from data center operators and utility customers across EMEA.

Source: Business Wire  ·  25 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

European Commission Proposes Mandatory A to G Sustainability Labels for Large Data Centers From August 2027, Requiring Operators to Disclose Energy and Water Efficiency, Grid Support and Waste Heat Recovery Performance

The European Commission proposed on 21 September that operators of data centers with a minimum capacity of 500 kilowatts will be required to display a sustainability label graded A to G from August 2027, modelled on the labelling scheme used for household appliances, covering energy use efficiency, water consumption relative to local stress levels, grid balancing support and contribution to waste heat recovery. The proposal does not set consumption limits at this stage; binding minimum performance standards are scheduled to follow in 2027 as part of a broader regulatory drive to improve the environmental footprint of European digital infrastructure.

Source: Euronews  ·  21 September 2026
 New Builds

Adani Group Wins Odisha State Approval for a Rs 1.04 Trillion, 1GW Integrated Data Center Park at Naraj in Cuttack, With 250 Acres Earmarked and Around 1,000 Jobs Promised

The Odisha government's High Level Clearance Authority, chaired by Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi, approved on 25 September a proposal from High Density Data Centre Ltd, a subsidiary of Adani Infra (India), to develop a 1 gigawatt integrated data center park across around 250 acres at Naraj in Cuttack district at an investment of Rs 1.04 trillion (approximately $12.4 billion). The approval makes this the second largest data center investment by value announced by an Indian conglomerate in 2026, behind Reliance Industries' planned Rs 1.08 trillion AI data center in Andhra Pradesh, and reflects the surge in sovereign AI infrastructure ambition across India's largest industrial groups.

Source: Business Standard  ·  25 September 2026
 New Builds

Macquarie Data Centres Completes the Structure of Its AUD $350 Million, 47MW IC3 Super West AI Facility in Sydney, the Only New AI Ready Capacity Opening in the City's Northern Zone in 2026

Macquarie Data Centres has topped out its IC3 Super West data center at the Macquarie Park campus in Sydney, marking the structural completion of a 47 megawatt facility built to support the high density power and liquid cooling demands of GPU and AI workloads, with the Australian Treasurer attending the ceremony and opening targeted for the third quarter of 2026. IC3 Super West, which supports both air cooling and direct to chip liquid cooling, is the only facility adding new AI ready capacity to Sydney's northern zone this year and reflects the growing appetite for purpose built sovereign digital infrastructure across Australia.

Source: Macquarie Data Centres  ·  September 2026
 New Builds

Crusoe Confirms It Is the Developer Behind Google's AI Data Center Campus in Armstrong County, Texas, Where More Than 5,500 Workers Are on Site Daily and Six Buildings Are Due for Completion by End of 2027

Crusoe announced on 28 September that it is the developer behind Google's data center campus under construction in Armstrong County outside Amarillo, Texas, where more than 5,500 skilled workers are on site daily following a groundbreaking in June 2025, with the first of six buildings due to be handed to Google in early 2027 and all construction expected to be complete by the end of that year. The campus connects directly to two adjacent Goodnight wind farms totalling 531 megawatts and uses a liquid cooling system that does not rely on evaporation, significantly limiting the facility's water footprint.

Source: GlobeNewswire  ·  28 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

US Department of Energy Commits $1.9 Billion in Federal Funding for 31 Grid Upgrade Projects Across 26 States to Unlock More Than 23GW of Additional Transmission Capacity for Data Centers and AI Infrastructure

The US Department of Energy announced on 24 September $1.9 billion in federal funding for 31 transmission improvement projects across 26 states under its SPARK initiative, with recipients committing a further $3.35 billion in matching contributions for a combined package of $5.25 billion intended to rebuild or replace more than 1,500 miles of transmission lines and unlock over 23 gigawatts of additional grid capacity. The investment is aimed at reducing the interconnection queues running to several years that have become a leading constraint on data center site selection and hyperscale build programmes across the United States.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  24 September 2026

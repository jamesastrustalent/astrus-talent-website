Capex Watch

Humain Plans $2.5 Billion Fund via BSF Capital to Finance 250MW of Saudi Data Centers

AI firm Humain, backed by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, is raising an initial $2.5 billion from global and domestic investors through a fund managed by BSF Capital, structured with a mix of debt and equity to finance 250 megawatts of data center capacity being built in partnership with Al Moammar Information Systems Co. The fund has scope to eventually support up to 1 gigawatt of capacity across the Kingdom, with Capital Market Authority approval of the vehicle expected within two to three months.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  3 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Gulf States Press Ahead with AI Data Center Plans Despite Drone Strikes on Cloud Infrastructure

The Gulf's wealthiest states are pushing forward with large scale AI data center plans even as regional conflict has made cloud infrastructure a military target, with drone strikes damaging Amazon Web Services facilities in the UAE and Bahrain in March and Iran's Revolutionary Guard subsequently naming 18 US technology companies operating in the region as legitimate targets. Saudi Arabia's operational data center capacity has grown from 68 megawatts in 2021 to more than 467 megawatts, and government backed firms in Abu Dhabi continue to advance a planned 5 gigawatt AI campus despite the heightened security environment.

Source: The Japan Times  ·  7 September 2026
 New Builds

Google Pledges €13 Billion for AI Data Centers in Finland in Its Largest Single European Investment

Google has announced it will invest at least €13 billion in data centers and digital infrastructure across four Finnish municipalities over 2027 and 2028, marking its largest ever single investment in Europe. The facilities in Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos and Vaala will power a range of Google services including its Gemini AI platform, with the company estimating the programme will add €3.6 billion per year to Finland's GDP and support more than 37,000 jobs during construction.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  9 September 2026
 New Builds

Greenfield and Finsbury Infrastructure Partner to Build 36MW AI Data Center East of Milan with €360 Million Investment

UK infrastructure developer Greenfield has formed a strategic partnership with Finsbury Infrastructure to develop a 36 megawatt data center east of Milan, with up to €360 million expected to be invested in the project and the surrounding region over three years. Greenfield has secured 80 megawatts of power capacity for the site, more than double the initial IT load, providing room to scale as AI and cloud demand across southern Europe continues to grow.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  7 September 2026
 Capex Watch

Asia Pacific Data Centre Pipeline Reaches Record 26.5GW in H1 2026 as Malaysia and Thailand Drive Southeast Asia Surge

Asia Pacific's data center development pipeline reached a record 26.5 gigawatts in the first half of 2026, comprising 4.8 gigawatts under construction and 21.7 gigawatts in planning, as the region added 7.1 gigawatts of new pipeline in just six months according to Cushman and Wakefield's H1 2026 update. Southeast Asia accounts for roughly half the under-construction capacity, with Malaysia leading at 1,039 megawatts and Bangkok's data center capacity surging 148 per cent to 859 megawatts, while colocation vacancy across the region fell from 10.9 per cent to 10.3 per cent as AI and cloud demand tightens supply.

Source: Cushman & Wakefield  ·  August 2026
 New Builds

Goodman Wins Approval for AUD 1.37 Billion, 135MW Data Centre at Macquarie Park in Northern Sydney

New South Wales planning authorities approved Goodman Group's AUD 1.37 billion state significant development application for a 135 megawatt data centre at Macquarie Park in northern Sydney on 2 September 2026, with eight data halls across four levels supported by large scale evaporative cooling and lithium ion battery backup systems. The project is expected to create up to 400 construction jobs and 60 permanent operational roles, and forms part of Goodman's global data center work in progress portfolio, which now exceeds $18 billion.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

California Passes Data Center Electricity Bills Requiring Operators to Bear Their Own Grid Costs

California's legislature passed two bills on 31 August 2026, the final day of its session, creating a dedicated electricity rate class for data centers and directing the California Public Utilities Commission to require operators to bear the cost of grid upgrades triggered by their connections, resolving what ratepayer advocates described as a $1.8 billion cost shifting risk. Assembly Bill 2383 and Senate Bill 886 now await Governor Gavin Newsom's signature or veto before the end of September, in what would be the most stringent state level data center power cost framework enacted in the United States.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Data Center Backlash Becomes Defining Bipartisan Issue Ahead of November US Midterm Elections

Public opposition to data centers has escalated from a niche local issue into a defining topic ahead of November's midterm elections, with a University of Pennsylvania poll finding that 61 per cent of Americans oppose new data centers in their communities, up 12 percentage points since early in the year. The backlash crosses party lines, with 75 per cent of Democrats and 63 per cent of Republicans opposing facilities near them, and at least 75 projects worth a combined $130 billion were blocked or delayed by community opposition in the first quarter of 2026 alone.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  9 September 2026

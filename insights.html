Oracle Opens New Data Centers in the UAE and Saudi Arabia as AI Cloud Demand Surges, With More Sites Planned in the Next Six Months

Oracle has added new data center locations in the UAE and Saudi Arabia over the past six months and plans further sites in the next six, a senior executive confirmed to The National on 29 October 2026. The company declines to disclose specific locations after a number of its Gulf facilities were previously targeted by Iran, though it confirmed its UAE capacity has grown substantially to support rising AI workloads.

Source: The National  ·  29 October 2026

Nebius Signs Preliminary Agreement to Take 50 MW at HUMAIN and DataVolt's Oxagon AI Campus in Neom, Saudi Arabia, Becoming the Project's First Commercial Customer

HUMAIN and DataVolt signed a preliminary framework agreement with Nvidia powered cloud infrastructure company Nebius to supply up to 50 MW at the first phase of the Oxagon AI campus in Neom on 21 October 2026, making Nebius the project's first commercial customer, Data Center Dynamics reported. Capacity is targeted for delivery from Q4 2027 in phases, within a first phase of 100 MW now under construction and a total campus target of 1.5 GW powered by Neom's own wind and solar assets.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  22 October 2026

Finnish Authorities Refer Google's Muhos Data Center Site to Police for Criminal Investigation After an Estimated 250,000 Trees Were Cleared Before Environmental Assessments Were Finalised

Finland's oversight authority asked police to open a criminal investigation into tree felling at Google's Muhos data center site, where an estimated 250,000 trees were cleared before required environmental impact assessments were complete, Data Center Dynamics reported on 30 October 2026. Google, whose Finnish programme is part of a €13 billion investment commitment, simultaneously purchased a further 56 hectares of land in Hamina, where it already operates an established data center facility.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  30 October 2026

KDDI Acquires the 1.1 Million Square Foot Inventory Estate in London's Docklands, a Site With Existing Planning Consent for a New Data Center Adjacent to the Telehouse Campus

KDDI, the Japanese telecommunications group and parent company of Telehouse Europe, has acquired the Inventory Estate, a 1.1 million square foot campus in London's Docklands that carries existing planning consent for a new data center facility, Data Center Dynamics reported on 29 October 2026. The acquisition positions Telehouse to develop a sixth data center on its established Docklands campus, one of Europe's most connected colocation hubs and a critical node in global internet exchange infrastructure.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  29 October 2026

NTT DATA Opens AI Factory Sites in Singapore and Paris, Expanding Its Global Network to Four Environments Where Enterprises Can Test and Scale AI Workloads Before Production

NTT DATA announced on 27 October 2026 that it had opened AI Factory sites in Singapore and Paris, joining existing facilities in Plano, Texas, and Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, to create a four site network where organisations can test, secure and govern AI workloads before committing to production scale deployments. Each site is built on a Cisco Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA foundation and combines NTT DATA consulting and managed services with infrastructure designed to take enterprise AI from pilots to production.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  29 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

NEXTDC Shares Fall 44% After Grid Operator Jemena Suspends Network Works to the Melbourne M3 Campus Following Community Opposition, Forcing a Cut to FY27 Guidance of More Than a Third

NEXTDC requested a trading halt on 19 October 2026 after grid operator Jemena suspended electricity network works needed to supply its M3 data center campus in Melbourne's inner west, citing community opposition. When the company disclosed the situation on 21 October it cut FY27 underlying EBITDA guidance by more than a third, paused all new development projects and land acquisitions, and saw its shares fall from A$9.18 to as low as A$5.07 on the day.

Source: Business News Australia  ·  21 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Voters in 10 US States Will Consider 41 Local Measures on Election Day to Restrict, Delay or Ban New Data Centers, With Ohio Leading the Nation With 18 Communities Voting on Limits

Voters in 38 jurisdictions across 10 states will consider at least 41 measures on 3 November 2026 that would impose new requirements on, pause or outright prohibit data center construction, as a bipartisan backlash against rapid AI driven development grows, the Detroit News reported on 29 October 2026. Ohio leads the nation with 18 communities voting, including Granville, Hubbard and Urbana where residents are considering outright bans; measures elsewhere range from zoning changes to mandatory voter approval requirements.

Source: Detroit News  ·  29 October 2026

Oregon Governor Tina Kotek Directs State Environmental Regulator to Pause New Air and Water Permits for Data Centers Until July 2027, Creating a De Facto Construction Moratorium

Governor Tina Kotek directed Oregon's Department of Environmental Quality to suspend new or renewed air and water pollution permits for data centers until July 2027, a step Data Center Dynamics described as a de facto moratorium, reported on 13 October 2026. Amazon warned immediately of job and investment losses, several counties including Umatilla declared emergencies over potential financial impacts, and Kotek said the pause gives time for her Data Center Advisory Committee's recommendations and possible 2027 legislation.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026

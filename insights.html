Trends, analysis, and perspectives from inside the Data Center talent market.

Last updated 14 October 2026

New Builds

Microsoft Sets November 2026 as the Launch Date for Its Saudi Arabia East Cloud Region in the Eastern Province, Comprising Three Azure Availability Zones and Marking the Company's First Native Cloud Region in the Kingdom for Sovereign Data Hosting

Microsoft confirmed on 31 August 2026 that its Saudi Arabia East cloud region will open to customers in November 2026, delivering three Azure availability zones in the Eastern Province to allow Saudi organisations to run cloud and AI workloads within the kingdom under local data residency requirements. Once live, the region will bring Microsoft's global Azure estate to more than 70 regions across 33 countries and will operate under the Microsoft Cloud for Sovereignty framework aligned with the Saudi National Data Governance Interim Regulation.

Source: Microsoft News  ·  31 August 2026
 Market Intelligence

UAE and Saudi Arabia Set to Account for More Than 80% of Gulf Data Center Expansion With GCC IT Power Capacity Forecast to Triple to Around 3GW by 2030, Even as the Region Contributes Less Than 2% of Net Global Capacity Growth

Analysis published by Fast Company Middle East on 13 October 2026 projects that GCC installed data center IT power will triple to roughly 3GW by 2030, with the UAE and Saudi Arabia together driving more than four fifths of new capacity on the back of national AI programmes, hyperscaler entry and improving grid access. Despite the pace of construction, combined UAE and Saudi additions are expected to represent under 2% of global IT power capacity additions between 2026 and 2030.

Source: Fast Company Middle East  ·  13 October 2026
 New Builds

Scaleway Plans Its First AI Supercomputer Deployment Outside France With GPU Clusters in Sweden Built on AMD Instinct MI355X Processors, Alongside a Further GPU Deployment in Copenhagen, Both Announced on 13 October 2026

French cloud provider Scaleway announced on 13 October 2026 that it will deploy GPU clusters in Sweden and Copenhagen, with the Swedish site set to become the company's first AI supercomputer outside France, built on AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs with Vast Data providing the storage platform and Tele2 supplying hosting capacity. Neither site has disclosed total GPU count, capacity or an expected live date.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Nscale Raises $3.36 Billion in Pre IPO Convertible Loan Notes Led by Third Point and NVIDIA to Fund Liquid Cooled Data Centers and GPU Cluster Expansion, With a $1 Billion NVIDIA Tranche Due in Mid November 2026 Ahead of a Planned NYSE Listing

Nscale announced on 25 September 2026 that it had closed a $3.36 billion raise via convertible loan notes, with Third Point leading and NVIDIA and more than a dozen institutional investors participating across a $2.36 billion first tranche at closing and a $1 billion NVIDIA commitment due in mid November 2026. The notes convert into shares on completion of Nscale's planned NYSE listing, and the capital is earmarked for power plant development, liquid cooled data centers and GPU cluster expansion across a pipeline the company says carries more than $103 billion in contracted value.

Source: TelecomTV  ·  25 September 2026
 Capex Watch

DayOne Closes $3.67 Billion in Multicurrency Financing From Seven Banks for Its Malaysian Data Center Expansion, Combining a $2.4 Billion Offshore Term Loan With a RM5.5 Billion Islamic Finance Facility to Fund Its Selangor Development Pipeline

DayOne announced on 13 October 2026 that a seven bank consortium including CIMB, Credit Agricole CIB, DBS, Maybank, OCBC, Standard Chartered and UOB had committed $3.67 billion in multicurrency financing split across a $2.4 billion offshore term loan and a RM5.5 billion Murabahah facility. The company operates 120MW across two facilities in Johor and is developing a further site in Selangor where it is exploring up to 1.5GW of on site generation with TNB Power Generation.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Asia Pacific's Data Center Development Pipeline Reaches a Record 26.5GW in the First Half of 2026 as Cushman and Wakefield Estimates More Than $280 Billion in Capital Will Be Required to Complete the Region's In Development Capacity Through to 2030

Cushman and Wakefield's H1 2026 data center market review found that the Asia Pacific development pipeline reached 26.5GW at the end of June, a record high underpinned by hyperscaler and sovereign AI spending across Malaysia, Japan, India and Australia. The firm projects that completing the existing pipeline through 2030 will require more than $280 billion in capital investment, highlighting both the scale of regional ambition and the challenge of financing, powering and connecting the next generation of facilities.

Source: Cushman and Wakefield / IREI  ·  2026
 Market Intelligence

FERC Approves PJM Interconnection's Proposal to Build a Data Center Registry Tracking Location, Power Providers and Load Ramp Timelines, Rejecting a Companion Provision That Would Have Authorised the Grid Operator to Curtail Power to Data Centers During Peak Demand

The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission approved PJM Interconnection's request on 13 October 2026 to establish a data center database recording each site's location, power suppliers, in service dates, load ramp timelines and backup generation arrangements, improving the RTO's visibility over a fast growing segment of its load. FERC declined to approve a separate PJM measure that would have allowed temporary curtailment of data center power during peak grid stress, finding that the proposed mechanism exceeded PJM's statutory authority as a regional transmission operator.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Oracle Is Trucking Compressed Natural Gas to Data Centers in Utah and Texas While Permanent Pipelines Are Built, at a Delivered Cost Around Four Times the Major Hub Price, and Is Weighing the Same Approach for Its Project Jupiter Campus in New Mexico

Bloomberg reported on 8 October 2026 that Oracle has been sourcing compressed natural gas via virtual pipeline providers Certarus and VoltaGrid to fuel its site near Salt Lake City in Utah and its OpenAI campus in Shackelford County, Texas, as permanent pipeline connections lag behind construction schedules. Oracle may extend the approach to Project Jupiter in New Mexico, where the state land office has twice refused rights of way for a planned gas pipeline and the connection date has slipped to February 2027, with Oracle having issued a force majeure notice to the site developer.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  8 October 2026

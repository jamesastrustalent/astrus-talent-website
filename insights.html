Market Intelligence

Oracle Expands Cloud Infrastructure Across the UAE and Saudi Arabia as AI Demand Accelerates, Keeping New Site Locations Confidential After Iranian Threats Against Gulf Facilities

Oracle told The National it is adding data centers in the UAE and Saudi Arabia to meet surging AI demand, with the company also eyeing Qatar and Kuwait, but it is declining to disclose the exact locations of new builds after Iran threatened Gulf data infrastructure earlier this year. Oracle already operates cloud regions in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Jeddah and Riyadh, with a Neom site listed as coming soon on its website.

Source: The National  ·  29 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

EQT Seeks Joint AI and Data Center Investments With Saudi and UAE Sovereign Investors, Planning to Expand Through Portfolio Firm EdgeConneX

Europe's largest private markets firm EQT told The National it is in discussions with sovereign investors in Saudi Arabia and the UAE about jointly investing in AI and data center infrastructure, with portfolio company EdgeConneX, which operates more than 106 data centers across 25 countries, expected to serve as the primary vehicle for any new Gulf builds. EQT opened a new office in Abu Dhabi to deepen relationships with sovereign funds, family offices and institutional partners in the region.

Source: The National  ·  3 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Over 100 Protesters Enter Microsoft Data Center Construction Site in Amsterdam as Coordinated Opposition to Data Center Growth Spreads Across 15 European Countries

Activists from the Dutch collective Geef Tegengas entered a Microsoft data center construction site in Amsterdam on 6 November 2026, with one person arrested, as part of a wider campaign that organised marches and protests in Dublin, Madrid, Milan and Edinburgh over the same weekend. Protesters cited Microsoft's contracts with immigration authorities and AI surveillance concerns, alongside the energy and water demands of large scale data center campuses.

Source: Data Center Dynamics / Bloomberg  ·  6 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Danish Parliament Adopts Emergency Electricity Grid Plan Placing Data Centers at the Back of the Connection Queue as Total Requests to Energinet Surpass 60 GW

Denmark's parliament approved emergency legislation in October 2026 requiring grid operator Energinet to prioritise other applicants over data centers in the queue for new electricity connections, after total connection requests reached more than 60 GW against a national peak demand of approximately 7 GW. The Danish Data Center Industry association stated that simply reshuffling the queue distributes but does not resolve the underlying power shortage.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  October 2026
 New Builds

Google Commits $15 Billion to Malaysian Data Center Expansion in 2027 and 2028, Adding New Campuses in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan and Pledging 1 GW of Clean Energy

Google announced on 4 November 2026 that it will spend $15 billion expanding its data center infrastructure in Malaysia during 2027 and 2028, with new campuses planned in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan alongside its existing Elmina Business Park facility and a separately committed $160 million for water infrastructure in Negeri Sembilan. As part of the investment Google also pledged to deliver 1 GW of clean energy capacity to Malaysia's national grid.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

DayOne and Blue Whale Energy Sign Agreement to Study a Distributed Virtual Power Plant at the Planned SG1 Data Center in Singapore

DayOne Data Centers and Blue Whale Energy signed a memorandum of understanding on 30 October 2026 to investigate whether battery storage at DayOne's upcoming 20 MW SG1 facility in Singapore could be linked with other commercial and industrial sites to operate as a single flexible grid resource. The partners also plan to explore participation in Singapore's demand response and interruptible load programmes, which reward large consumers for reducing power use during periods of grid stress.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  30 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Newly Elected Governors in Ohio, Georgia, Michigan, New Mexico and Texas Plan to Pause or Stop Data Center Construction, Threatening Around 40 Percent of Total US Planned Capacity

Bloomberg reported on 6 November 2026 that newly elected governors in five states all campaigned on promises of stopping or pausing data center construction, with analyst firm Cleanview estimating that approximately 40 percent of the country's total planned data center capacity sits within those states. A further 18 percent of planned capacity is located in states where incoming governors pledged to revise tax incentives and permitting rules rather than impose an outright moratorium.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  6 November 2026
 New Builds

Google Eyes Approximately 1,000 Acres in Goochland County, Virginia, for a 1.2 GW Data Center Campus at West Creek Business Park

Goochland County released documents at the end of October 2026 confirming that Google is evaluating a site of roughly 1,000 acres at West Creek Business Park west of Richmond for a data center campus of approximately 1.2 GW capacity; the site sits within a technology overlay district where data center development is permitted as of right and does not require a public hearing. Google had submitted no formal plans as of the date of the county's release and the project remains at the due diligence stage.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  October 2026

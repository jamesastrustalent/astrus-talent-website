Capex Watch

Humain Secures 211 Land Plots Across Saudi Arabia in Record Land Drive

State backed AI venture Humain has secured 211 plots of land across Saudi Arabia through a coordinated effort involving 16 government entities, unlocking access to more than 14GW of potential capacity. The programme advances Saudi Arabia's goal of building approximately 6GW of data center capacity over the coming decade.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  August 2026
 New Builds

Khazna Data Centers Opens Two AI Ready Facilities in Abu Dhabi Delivering 60MW of New Capacity

Khazna Data Centers, the data center arm of Abu Dhabi's G42, has launched two new facilities at Masdar City and Mafraq, adding a combined 60MW of capacity designed for AI and cloud workloads using adiabatic free cooling and modular architecture built to LEED Gold standards. The openings form part of a wider programme to scale sovereign AI infrastructure across the UAE to meet rapidly growing demand from hyperscalers and government customers.

Source: Aletihad  ·  August 2026
 Capex Watch

Microsoft Signs $6.2 Billion Five Year AI Compute Deal with Nscale Powered by Norwegian Renewables

Microsoft has contracted $6.2 billion of AI computing capacity from Nscale and Norwegian investment firm Aker ASA, to be delivered from a renewably powered data center campus in Norway with up to 520MW of capacity over a five year term starting in 2026. The deal lifts the combined value of Microsoft's agreements with Nscale to $14 billion across sites in Norway, the UK, Portugal and Texas.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2026
 Market Intelligence

EUDCA Forecasts €176 Billion in European Data Center Investment Through 2031 as Grid Constraints Tighten

The European Data Centre Association forecasts cumulative investment of €176 billion across Europe between 2026 and 2031, driven by a 17% compound annual growth rate in IT power demand, but warns that grid availability rather than capital will be the primary barrier to capacity growth. Development is rapidly decentralising beyond the traditional Frankfurt, London, Amsterdam, Paris and Dublin hubs as operators seek power and land in secondary markets.

Source: Data Center Knowledge  ·  2026
 Market Intelligence

Asia Pacific Data Center Pipeline Hits Record 26.5GW as AI Demand Accelerates

Asia Pacific's data center development pipeline reached a record 26.5GW in H1 2026, adding 7.1GW in just six months according to Cushman and Wakefield's latest report. Johor leads the region with over 4GW of combined operational and planned capacity, while tight vacancy rates in Tokyo and Singapore signal continued strong demand.

Source: Cushman & Wakefield  ·  August 2026
 New Builds

CoreWeave Plans 360MW Indonesia Expansion as First Move into Asia Pacific

CoreWeave has announced plans for three data centers in Greater Jakarta delivering a combined 360MW of contracted IT power, marking the company's first entry into Asia Pacific and its largest geographic expansion since its founding. The facilities are expected to come online in 2028 and will serve AI companies, cloud users and government customers across Southeast Asia under a model where CoreWeave owns and operates the compute environment.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 August 2026
 Capex Watch

Nvidia Backs OpenAI Ohio Data Center Campus with Up to $105 Billion Guarantee Covering 8GW of Capacity

Nvidia has agreed to guarantee up to $105 billion to support OpenAI's planned data center campus in Pike County, Ohio, covering an agreement for up to 8GW of computing capacity with the first 800MW expected online by 2028. Developer SB Energy will build and operate the campus under a 20 year lease with OpenAI, establishing one of the largest AI infrastructure commitments yet announced in the United States.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  17 August 2026
 Market Intelligence

Up to Half of Planned US Data Centers Face Delays as Political Backlash and Infrastructure Complexity Mount

Energy investment firm Kimmeridge warns that as many as half of proposed US data center projects are at risk of delay or cancellation, driven by growing political opposition, permitting complexity and supply chain constraints on physical infrastructure. The firm expects slowdowns to weigh on US gas demand forecasts even as developers press ahead with new power plant construction to serve AI workloads.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  26 August 2026

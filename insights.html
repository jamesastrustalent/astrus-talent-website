Capex Watch

Microsoft Commits $10 Billion to Gulf Cloud and AI Infrastructure Through 2030, Adding $2 Billion in New Funding Across Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar and the UAE

Microsoft announced on 23 September a total commitment of $10 billion in cloud and AI infrastructure spending across four Gulf states through 2030, including $2 billion in freshly pledged funds building on the $7.9 billion previously earmarked for the UAE. The company also confirmed plans to invest more than $400 million in subsea and terrestrial connectivity across the Middle East and to prioritise zero water cooling technologies for new data centers in the region where feasible.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  23 September 2026
 Capex Watch

Alibaba Cloud Announces First Cloud Regions in Turkey, Finland and the Netherlands and Expanded Data Center Capacity in the UAE, Germany, France, Malaysia and Hong Kong as Part of Its 20 Gigawatt Global Buildout

Alibaba Cloud revealed plans at its annual Apsara Conference on 23 September to establish its first cloud regions in Turkey, Finland and the Netherlands within 12 months and to expand capacity in the UAE, Germany, France, Malaysia and Hong Kong, bringing its footprint to more than 30 regions and over 107 availability zones worldwide. The expansion deepens competition with US hyperscalers across European and Middle Eastern enterprise and government markets as Alibaba accelerates its global AI infrastructure strategy.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  23 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

European Commission Proposes Mandatory Sustainability Rating Scheme for Data Centers Above 500 Kilowatts, With Automatically Generated Electronic Labels Due From August 2027

The European Commission on 21 September proposed a common EU rating framework that will assign annually renewed electronic labels to data centers with a capacity above 500 kilowatts, disclosing performance across four indicators: power usage effectiveness, water usage effectiveness, energy reuse factor and renewable energy factor. The scheme is subject to a two month scrutiny period by the European Parliament and Council before taking effect, with a consultation on minimum performance standards open until 14 December 2026.

Source: European Commission  ·  21 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Vertiv Agrees to Acquire Ireland Based King Environmental Services, Extending Its Fluid Management and Commissioning Capabilities for Liquid Cooled Data Centers Across EMEA and Global Markets

Vertiv announced on 24 September that it has entered into an agreement to acquire King Environmental Services, an Ireland based provider of fluid management, commissioning and load testing services for high density liquid cooled data centers, with the transaction expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026. The acquisition extends Vertiv's service capabilities across the full thermal chain in EMEA as demand accelerates for specialist commissioning and testing expertise to support large scale liquid cooling deployments driven by AI workloads.

Source: PR Newswire  ·  24 September 2026
 New Builds

Anthropic Signs Agreement for AUD 32 Billion Queensland Data Center Campus of 2.16 Gigawatts Near Dalby, Confirming Australia's Largest Proposed Data Center Project to Date

Anthropic has signed its first Australian data center agreement with Zerra DC for the Western Downs Digital Park, a planned 2.16 gigawatt campus on 725 hectares of farmland approximately 250 kilometres west of Brisbane, at an estimated total value of AUD 32 billion. The deal, confirmed in Queensland State Parliament on 16 September by Premier David Crisafulli, is subject to Foreign Investment Review Board approval and envisages first capacity from 2027 across a four to six year phased construction schedule supporting approximately 1,500 construction jobs and 1,400 ongoing operational roles.

Source: ABC News  ·  16 September 2026
 Capex Watch

EQT Plans $50 Billion India Investment by 2030 With $30 Billion for Data Centers via Equal EdgeConneX and Adani Enterprises Joint Venture

Swedish infrastructure investor EQT announced at a media briefing in Mumbai on 16 September plans to commit $50 billion in India by 2030, allocating approximately $30 billion to data center infrastructure through portfolio company EdgeConneX under an equal joint venture with Adani Enterprises, and a further $5 billion to renewable energy to power the sites. The programme positions EQT and EdgeConneX among the largest committed investors in Indian AI data center capacity, targeting hyperscaler and domestic cloud demand as the country's compute requirements accelerate.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  16 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Research Finds 45 US Data Center Projects Worth $68 Billion Were Blocked or Delayed by Community Opposition in the Second Quarter of 2026

Research group Data Center Watch has found that 45 data center projects with a combined value of $68 billion were blocked or delayed by community opposition in the United States between April and June 2026, as localities pushed through moratoriums on new construction ahead of planning applications and 30 state legislatures introduced or adopted rules on data center siting, energy usage and water consumption. There are now 843 organised opposition groups operating across 49 states, with one petition against a Tennessee project gathering more than 500,000 signatures during the quarter alone.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  21 September 2026
 New Builds

Applied Digital Confirms $3.2 Billion Delta Forge 2 AI Data Center Campus in Brookwood, Alabama, on 1,300 Acres With Construction Starting October 2026 and Completion Targeted by December 2028

Applied Digital announced on 25 September that its Delta Forge 2 AI facility will be built near Brookwood in Tuscaloosa County, Alabama, covering 1,300 acres with 1.2 million square feet of capacity at full build out, with construction scheduled to begin on 1 October 2026 and a target completion date of December 2028. Tuscaloosa County approved incentive packages for the project and Applied Digital committed $270 million in community benefits payments; the unnamed hyperscale tenant, described as investment grade, is leasing the capacity through Kayvens LLC and is expected to create 100 permanent operational roles.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  25 September 2026

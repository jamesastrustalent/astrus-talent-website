Trends, analysis, and perspectives from inside the Data Center talent market.

New Builds

Humain Issues a Request for Proposals for a 100 MW AI Data Center EPC Contract in Riyadh's Al Saad Area Worth an Estimated $1 Billion, With Bids Due 8 November and Phase One Completion Targeted for October 2028

The contract covers a facility designed to support rack densities of 240 to 250 kW per rack for high density GPU clusters, with an optional second phase that would raise total site capacity to 200 MW. The tender marks a continuation of Humain's rapid procurement programme in Saudi Arabia as the kingdom scales its AI infrastructure base.

Source: MEED  ·  18 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Abu Dhabi Sets a Target of 100% Carbon Free Data Center Cooling Power by 2035 as the Market Accelerates Capacity Expansion and Khazna Deploys AI Driven Thermal Controls in Partnership With the UAE Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure

Gulf News reported on 14 October 2026 that local operators achieved a 27% carbon free share of cooling load in 2025 and are targeting full elimination of fossil fuel use in cooling systems within a decade. The initiative involves AI driven energy management developed in collaboration with Agility and UAE government energy bodies.

Source: Gulf News  ·  14 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Microsoft Withdraws as Anchor Tenant From Nscale's Loughton Essex AI Data Center Project After National Grid Delays Push Power Availability to the Early to Mid 2030s, Highlighting UK Grid Constraints on Hyperscale Development

Reported on 19 October 2026, Microsoft's departure leaves Nscale exploring alternative power sources and UK sites after its principal customer confirmed it could not wait a decade for grid access. The withdrawal is the latest in a series of UK grid connection setbacks affecting planned data center developments in the London commuter belt.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  19 October 2026
 New Builds

French Consortium Aether Infrastructure Acquires a Former Gearbox Factory in Strasbourg to Develop FR SXB1, Its First AI Data Center With 25 MW at Opening in 2027 and a Long Term 400 MW Campus Across Two Sites Seeking EU AI Gigafactory Funding

Announced on 14 October 2026, the facility will be built in Strasbourg's Port du Rhin district by a consortium including SiPearl, 2CRSi, Nhood and Dassault Systèmes. A second Strasbourg campus is planned, with combined capacity rising to 400 MW subject to European Union funding support under the AI gigafactory programme.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  14 October 2026
 New Builds

NTT Data in Final Talks to Build a 500 MW $6.3 Billion Dedicated AI Data Center for Anthropic in Osaka Prefecture, Marking Anthropic's First Owned Facility in Japan With Partial Operations Targeted From 2028

Reported by Nikkei Asia and covered by Data Center Dynamics on 13 October 2026, the deal would see NTT Data construct and own the facility at a cost of up to one trillion yen with Anthropic as sole occupant. Neither company had made a public confirmation at the time of reporting.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Princeton Digital Group Divests Its Five Enterprise Data Center Sites in Indonesia to PT SMPlus Digital Investama Across Greater Jakarta, Bandung, Surabaya and Pekanbaru to Focus Exclusively on Its 400 MW Hyperscale Campus Portfolio

Announced on 12 October 2026, the divestment covers all of PDG's non hyperscale assets in Indonesia, with the company retaining its JC2, JC3 and JC4 campuses totalling approximately 400 MW. PDG described the sale as a strategic refocus on large scale AI and cloud infrastructure at a point when enterprise colocation growth is being outpaced by hyperscale demand.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  12 October 2026
 New Builds

Taylor City Council Unanimously Approves the 664 Acre Taylor Technology Campus, a 1 GW Behind the Meter Joint Venture Between PowerHouse Data Centers and Big Watt Digital Backed by a Dedicated 750 MW Natural Gas Plant With First Power in 2028

Approved on approximately 13 October 2026, phase one of the campus will deliver 500 MW with first power available in the first half of 2028, with a further 500 MW to follow in subsequent phases. At full build out the campus would rank among the largest single site data center developments approved in the United States this year.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  13 October 2026
 Capex Watch

NiSource Signs a 20 Year Power Supply Agreement With Microsoft to Deliver 1,100 MW to a New Data Center Campus in North West Indiana Starting in 2028, With an Estimated $1.75 Billion in Projected Savings for Local Electricity Customers

Announced on 16 October 2026, Microsoft will cover generation and transmission costs through NiSource's GenCo subsidiary, with a portion of the infrastructure investment structured to return savings to residential and commercial ratepayers. The arrangement signals a new model for hyperscale power procurement in which utilities structure data center supply deals with direct ratepayer benefits to smooth regulatory approval.

Source: NiSource  ·  16 October 2026

Market Intelligence

Abu Dhabi Signs Four Agreements With OpenAI as Sam Altman Meets Crown Prince Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed, Covering Energy, Government Services, Investment and Education Through ADNOC and G42

Sam Altman visited Abu Dhabi on 2 November 2026 and four deals were signed by emirate entities including ADNOC, which became the first energy company to deploy OpenAI's frontier models for operational use, and investment vehicle Inception42, a G42 unit applying AI to portfolio analysis and risk assessment. The agreements form part of a broader Abu Dhabi push to deepen AI infrastructure ties with US partners as sentiment towards data center construction turns negative across American states following the midterm elections.

Source: CNBC  ·  2 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN Acquires Riyadh Based Cloud and AI Services Firm CNTXT, Extending Its AI Platform to More Than 400 Enterprise and Government Customers Across the Kingdom

HUMAIN, the AI company jointly owned by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund and Aramco, announced on 3 November 2026 that it had acquired CNTXT, a Riyadh based cloud and managed services firm that serves more than 400 corporate and government clients as Google Cloud's reseller in Saudi Arabia and whose revenue has grown more than fourfold in the past three years. The deal extends HUMAIN beyond infrastructure and model development into enterprise delivery of AI and cloud services at scale.

Source: Arab News  ·  3 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

More Than 100 Protesters Break Into Microsoft Data Center Construction Site in Amsterdam With Police Detaining Around 150 People in the Most Dramatic European Action Yet Against AI Infrastructure

Demonstrators occupied a Microsoft data center construction site in Amsterdam's Sloterdijk district on 6 November 2026, with police detaining approximately 150 people, as the action formed part of coordinated Stop Dirty Data Centres protests across more than 15 countries that same week. Campaigners cited Microsoft's contracts with US ICE and the Israeli military alongside water and energy concerns, and accused the company of circumventing the Netherlands' nationwide ban on large hyperscale development by splitting its Amsterdam project into three smaller facilities.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  6 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Bank of England Asks UK Lenders to Disclose Exposure to Speculative Data Center Loans Tied to Projects With No Signed Tenants as Its Financial Policy Committee Flags AI Linked Lending Risks

The Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee sent letters to UK lenders in early November 2026 asking them to quantify their exposure to data center projects financed before any tenant has agreed to pay rent, following a warning about AI linked financing arrangements the committee described as carrying leverage, opacity and circular structures. A Bank survey found that four lenders had stopped lending to data centers entirely and that debt funds were supplying almost half of all speculative finance in the sector during the first half of 2026.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  5 November 2026
 New Builds

Princeton Digital Group Closes $1.3 Billion Green Loan Underwritten by Six Banks to Fund Its 198.8 MW JH1 Data Center Campus in Johor, Malaysia

Singapore headquartered operator Princeton Digital Group finalised $1.3 billion in green financing for its Johor campus on 4 November 2026, with the facility underwritten by Affin Bank, HSBC, Maybank, OCBC, Standard Chartered and UOB as part of the company's plan to raise up to $5 billion in total debt this year to fund contracted capacity across the Asia Pacific region. Malaysian authorities have separately approved a new requirement for all new data centers in the country to source a minimum of 30 per cent of their power from renewable energy.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 November 2026
 New Builds

Google Commits $15 Billion to Expand Its Malaysian Data Center Infrastructure During 2027 and 2028, Adding Campuses in Selangor and Negeri Sembilan and Pledging 1 GW of Clean Energy to the National Grid

Google confirmed on 4 November 2026 that it will invest $15 billion across two new data center campuses in Malaysia during 2027 and 2028, one in Selangor and one in Negeri Sembilan, alongside a separately committed $160 million for a water treatment facility in Negeri Sembilan and a pledge to deliver 1 GW of clean energy capacity to the national grid. The announcement reinforces Malaysia as a Southeast Asian anchor market for hyperscale infrastructure at a moment when the country has introduced a new 30 per cent renewable energy requirement for data center operators.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 November 2026
 New Builds

Google Commits $17 Billion to Its First Wyoming Data Center Campus Near Cheyenne, Powered by a 702 MW Fuel Cell Facility and Cooled by Air Rather Than Water to Address Community Concerns

Google announced on 5 November 2026 that it will spend $17 billion on a 716 acre data center campus in Laramie County near Cheyenne, its first facility in Wyoming, with power supplied through a 702 MW fuel cell deployment and air cooling chosen to minimise water draw from local aquifers. The company simultaneously acquired the site from Crusoe, which had stepped back as developer in June over cost and schedule concerns, and announced a Community Council at a moment when voters in 38 of 39 finalised US ballot measures had just enacted new restrictions on data center construction.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  5 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

OpenAI Drops NDAs for Public Officials and Publishes National Community Compact Promising Annual Transparency Reports at Every Data Center Site Following Midterm Backlash

OpenAI announced on 5 November 2026 that it would stop requiring public officials to sign nondisclosure agreements when negotiating data center deals and published a National Community Compact committing to individual community agreements at each site, annual public transparency reports and direct payment of electricity costs for facilities it owns and operates. The announcement came days after voters backed new data center restrictions in 38 of 39 counted ballot measures across the United States, and was accompanied by a broader Compute Campus Policy calling on federal and state governments to set industry wide transparency rules.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  5 November 2026

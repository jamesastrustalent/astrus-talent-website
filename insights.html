Capex Watch

Microsoft Commits More Than $10 Billion to Gulf Cloud and AI Infrastructure Across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait and Qatar Through 2030, Including a New $2 Billion Tranche Extending the Programme Beyond Its Existing UAE Commitment

Microsoft Vice Chair Brad Smith announced on 23 September that the company would invest more than $10 billion across four Gulf states through 2030, incorporating the $7.9 billion previously pledged for the UAE and extending the programme by approximately $2 billion to cover Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Qatar, with more than $400 million earmarked for undersea and land connectivity across West Asia. The package also includes skills training for 4.2 million people and encompasses the Saudi Arabia East Azure region in the Eastern Province, scheduled to open in November 2026 with three availability zones designed to support local data residency requirements.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  23 September 2026
 New Builds

Dammam Based Construction Firm Al Yamama Wins the Civil Infrastructure Contract for Humain's 6GW AI Data Center Campus in Riyadh, Covering Power Distribution at 380kV, 132kV and 33kV, Two Major Substations and a 2,000 MVA Bulk Supply Point Across a 24 Square Kilometre Site in East Riyadh

Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund backed AI company Humain has awarded the site infrastructure contract for its 6GW Riyadh data center campus to Al Yamama, with the development planned across six plots each targeting 1GW of capacity in the Al Saad district of east Riyadh. The contract scope covers high voltage power distribution at multiple voltage levels, two large substations, stormwater drainage, a sewage treatment plant, roads and underground fibre, and will be delivered via an early contractor involvement model.

Source: Enterprise AM  ·  27 September 2026
 New Builds

CX Tech Submits a £16.5 Billion Planning Application for a 1GW Data Center and Technology Campus at the Former Chapelcross Nuclear Power Station Site Near Annan in South West Scotland, Comprising Eight Buildings Across 162,000 Square Metres Powered Exclusively by Renewable Energy

Developer CX Tech lodged an application with Dumfries and Galloway Council for a 55 hectare campus on land north of the Chapelcross nuclear station, decommissioned in 2004, featuring eight data center buildings, a training centre, industrial space and direct wire connections to nearby wind, solar and biomass generation assets, with non evaporative air cooling specified to minimise water consumption. The company says the scheme would create approximately 750 construction jobs and around 500 permanent roles, with construction potentially running from 2027 to 2035 subject to planning consent.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2 October 2026
 Capex Watch

SoftBank Completes Its $3.1 Billion All Cash Acquisition of DigitalBridge, Taking the Digital Infrastructure Investor Private as a Controlled Subsidiary With CEO Marc Ganzi Continuing to Lead a Portfolio Spanning More Than $108 Billion in Assets Under Management

SoftBank closed its purchase of all outstanding DigitalBridge common stock at $16.00 per share on 1 October 2026, completing a deal first announced in December 2025 and approved by approximately 96% of shareholders in April 2026, with DigitalBridge to continue operating as a separately managed platform under SoftBank ownership. The firm's portfolio includes stakes in AIMS, AtlasEdge, DataBank, Switch, Vantage Data Centers and Yondr Group, spanning data centers, cell towers, fibre networks and edge infrastructure across Europe, North America and Asia Pacific.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 October 2026
 Capex Watch

Taiwanese AI Cloud Provider GMI Cloud Raises $668 Million Through a Series B Equity Round and Credit Facility With NVIDIA Participation to Scale GPU Capacity Across the United States, Taiwan and Asia Pacific as Contracted Annual Recurring Revenue Reaches More Than Nine Times Its Year End 2025 Level

GMI Cloud secured $223 million in Series B equity led by ARCHIV alongside a $445 million credit facility led by CTBC Bank, with NVIDIA, DSC Investment, Trend Micro, KB Investment, Kyobo Life and KT Corporation also participating, with funds earmarked for capacity expansion in the United States, Taiwan and the broader Asia Pacific region and the development of inference services. The company currently processes approximately 4 trillion tokens weekly for enterprise clients including Fireworks, Nous Research and Trend Micro, and reported live annual recurring revenue growing more than 4.5 times year on year.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Tencent Signs a Five Year Agreement With Oracle Worth Approximately $7 Billion to Lease 100,000 AI Graphics Processing Units Hosted Across Oracle Data Centers in Southeast Asia, the Largest Overseas Cloud Compute Lease Deal by a Chinese Technology Company on Record

The arrangement gives Tencent access to GPU clusters located outside mainland China, enabling the company to work around US export controls on advanced AI hardware, with Tencent paying roughly 30% of the total sum upfront and planning to use the capacity initially for training its Hunyuan large language models before expanding into inference workloads and bare metal and model as a service offerings. Oracle confirmed the deal and the companies described the arrangement as a multi year strategic partnership covering compute access at Oracle data center sites across the Southeast Asia region.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 October 2026
 New Builds

Startup Valar Atomics Files an Application to Build a 9.6GW Nuclear Powered Data Center Campus Called Project Beehive on Over 9,000 Acres of Bureau of Land Management Territory Near Price in Carbon County, Utah, Featuring 456 Small Modular Reactors With the First Units Targeted to Come Online in 2028

Valar Atomics applied to the Bureau of Land Management for a site approximately 199 miles south east of Salt Lake City, proposing a campus that would integrate data center buildings with more than 450 small nuclear reactors, an on site nuclear fuel production facility and nuclear waste storage, with full build out expected by 2032. The Bureau of Land Management's Utah office confirmed receipt of the application and said it would assess the proposal through the National Environmental Policy Act process with public comment opportunities as the review proceeds.

Source: NPR  ·  30 September 2026
 New Builds

Crusoe Files Planning Applications for Two Data Center Buildings Totalling Approximately 1.5 Million Square Feet in Jayton, Kent County, Texas, as a $4.8 Billion Satellite Campus of Project Hyper, Bringing the Full Programme Alongside Its Childress Development to Approximately $12 Billion

The Denver based AI compute developer filed for two data center buildings on County Road 233 and P2390 in Kent County, each measuring around 759,000 square feet, with construction targeted to begin in late January 2027 and the buildings expected to be operational in May and July 2029 respectively. The Jayton filings are described as spur developments of Project Hyper, centred on a larger campus in Childress, Armstrong County, to which Crusoe has committed $2.4 billion as part of the broader $12 billion programme across both Texas locations.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  28 September 2026

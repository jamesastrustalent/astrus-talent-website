Capex Watch

Humain Plans $2.5 Billion Fund via BSF Capital to Finance 250MW of Saudi Data Centers

AI firm Humain, backed by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, is raising an initial $2.5 billion from global and domestic investors through a fund managed by BSF Capital, structured with a mix of debt and equity to finance 250 megawatts of data center capacity being built in partnership with Al Moammar Information Systems Co. The fund has scope to eventually support up to 1 gigawatt of capacity across the Kingdom, with Capital Market Authority approval of the vehicle expected within two to three months.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  3 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

UAE Redesigns Its 5 Gigawatt Stargate AI Campus Into a Distributed Network After Iranian Drone Strikes

The United Arab Emirates is redesigning its planned 5 gigawatt Stargate UAE AI campus, originally envisaged as a single 10 square mile site in Abu Dhabi, into a distributed network of data centers spread across multiple emirates. Authorities are considering underground facilities in Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah and adding air defence systems, following Iranian missile and drone strikes that damaged two Amazon Web Services data centers in the UAE and one in Bahrain in March 2026.

Source: Reuters  ·  11 September 2026
 New Builds

Google Pledges €13 Billion for AI Data Centers in Finland in Its Largest Single European Investment

Google has announced it will invest at least €13 billion in data centers and digital infrastructure across four Finnish municipalities over 2027 and 2028, marking its largest ever single investment in Europe. The facilities in Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos and Vaala will power a range of Google services including its Gemini AI platform, with the company estimating the programme will add €3.6 billion per year to Finland's GDP and support more than 37,000 jobs during construction.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  9 September 2026
 New Builds

Cerebras Partners With Compute Nordic Finland on a 165 Megawatt AI Data Center Campus in Mikkeli

Cerebras Systems has signed a series of seven year service agreements with Compute Nordic Finland for a purpose built AI data center campus in Mikkeli, with the initial 50 megawatt phase already under construction and scope to expand to 165 megawatts of contracted IT capacity. At full scale the project represents an indicative investment of €1.0 to 1.7 billion for the region, supporting 80 to 250 permanent jobs and providing Cerebras with long term infrastructure to serve growing global demand for its AI compute platform.

Source: GlobeNewswire  ·  1 September 2026
 Capex Watch

CoreWeave Enters Asia Pacific With 360 Megawatt Indonesia Build Across Three Greater Jakarta Sites

CoreWeave has announced its first data center footprint in the Asia Pacific region, with three facilities planned across Greater Jakarta totalling 360 megawatts of contracted IT power, expected to come online in 2028. The expansion marks the AI cloud platform's first move beyond North America and Europe, bringing purpose built GPU compute infrastructure to laboratories, startups and enterprise customers across Southeast Asia.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 August 2026
 New Builds

Goodman Wins Approval for AUD 1.37 Billion, 135MW Data Centre at Macquarie Park in Northern Sydney

New South Wales planning authorities approved Goodman Group's AUD 1.37 billion state significant development application for a 135 megawatt data center at Macquarie Park in northern Sydney on 2 September 2026, with eight data halls across four levels supported by large scale evaporative cooling and lithium ion battery backup systems. The project is expected to create up to 400 construction jobs and 60 permanent operational roles, and forms part of Goodman's global data center work in progress portfolio, which now exceeds $18 billion.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  2 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

California Passes Data Center Electricity Bills Requiring Operators to Bear Their Own Grid Costs

California's legislature passed two bills on 31 August 2026, the final day of its session, creating a dedicated electricity rate class for data centers and directing the California Public Utilities Commission to require operators to bear the cost of grid upgrades triggered by their connections, resolving what ratepayer advocates described as a $1.8 billion cost shifting risk. Assembly Bill 2383 and Senate Bill 886 now await Governor Gavin Newsom's signature or veto before the end of September, in what would be the most stringent state level data center power cost framework enacted in the United States.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  1 September 2026
 Capex Watch

Microsoft Plans to Triple Data Center Capacity to 38 Gigawatts by 2032 in Largest AI Infrastructure Push

Microsoft has announced plans to expand its global network of data centers from roughly 12 gigawatts of capacity today to more than 38 gigawatts by 2032, adding approximately 26 gigawatts of new compute capacity with a growing share dedicated to AI specific accelerator hardware. The expansion programme is designed to overcome a computing shortage that has forced the company to turn away some AI and cloud customers, with AI chip capacity expected to grow from around 2 gigawatts currently to approximately one third of the 2032 total.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  10 September 2026

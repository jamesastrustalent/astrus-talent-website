Capex Watch

Microsoft Announces an Additional $2 Billion Investment Across the Gulf, Taking Its Total Commitment to Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait to More Than $10 Billion Through 2030, Alongside $400 Million for Regional Subsea Connectivity

Microsoft announced on 23 September, on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly, that it will commit an additional $2 billion to cloud and AI infrastructure across the Gulf, taking its total pledged spend in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar and Kuwait to more than $10 billion by 2030. The announcement also includes $400 million for subsea and terrestrial connectivity across the Middle East and is designed to extend Microsoft's data center and sovereign cloud footprint across every GCC market ahead of the launch of its Saudi Arabia East Azure region in November 2026.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  23 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Saudi State Backed AI Firm HUMAIN Seeks to Raise a $2.5 Billion Investment Vehicle via BSF Capital to Finance 250MW of Data Center Capacity, Signalling a Shift Toward Private Funding for the Kingdom's AI Infrastructure

HUMAIN, the Public Investment Fund backed entity leading Saudi Arabia's AI infrastructure programme, is seeking to raise a $2.5 billion investment vehicle managed by BSF Capital, the investment arm of Banque Saudi Fransi, with proceeds earmarked for 250 megawatts of data center capacity being developed alongside Al Moammar Information Systems and room to grow to 1 gigawatt. The move marks a significant shift from state capital toward private investment vehicles as Saudi Arabia targets 6 gigawatts of AI data center capacity by 2034.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  3 September 2026
 New Builds

Nabiax Breaks Ground on Its Third Alcalá Data Center Outside Madrid, a 100MW Campus Representing a Nearly €800 Million Investment That Will Be Built in Phases Through 2029 for Hyperscale and AI Workloads

Spanish data center operator Nabiax held a groundbreaking ceremony in Alcalá de Henares in September 2026 for Alcalá Data Center 3, a 100 megawatt facility built in stages through 2029 using a closed loop cooling system and designed to serve hyperscale and AI demand. Once fully operational, ADC3 will bring Nabiax's total installed IT power in Spain to 135 megawatts and is expected to generate around 1,000 construction jobs and more than 200 permanent roles.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  September 2026
 Market Intelligence

VIRTUS Data Centres Completes a £2.45 Billion Financing Package With 13 Banks to Fund UK and European Expansion, Including a £1.2 Billion Green Capital Expenditure Facility

VIRTUS Data Centres announced on 24 September the completion of a £2.45 billion financing package arranged with a consortium of 13 banks, with BNP Paribas, Crédit Agricole CIB, Société Générale and Standard Chartered acting as coordinators, representing one of the largest data center bank financings completed in the UK to date. Proceeds will fund the development of the company's 78 megawatt Saunderton campus in Buckinghamshire, its LONDON19 site in Slough and its ongoing expansion into continental Europe including the Marienpark campus in Berlin.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  24 September 2026
 New Builds

Amazon Agrees to Acquire Two Data Centers in Hong Kong for HK$2.18 Billion and Signs a Conditional Agreement to Buy 184,000 Square Metres of Freehold Land Near Kuala Lumpur, Both Deals Announced on 29 September

Amazon Data Services Hong Kong signed agreements on 29 September to acquire two nine storey properties in Hong Kong, offering combined capacity for 1,000 server racks, for a minimum gross consideration of HK$2.18 billion (approximately US$278 million), while its Malaysian subsidiary simultaneously agreed to acquire around 184,000 square metres of freehold land in Sepang, Selangor, roughly 40 kilometres south of Kuala Lumpur, for RM316.84 million ($77.6 million). The two transactions on the same day underscore the pace of Amazon Web Services' infrastructure expansion across Southeast Asia and greater China as AI and cloud demand continues to outpace existing capacity.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  29 September 2026
 New Builds

Goodman Group Raises US$455 Million for Its Hong Kong Data Centre Partnership to Fund the Fit Out of a 50MW Facility in Tsuen Wan, With Institutional Investors Including PGGM, APG and CPPIB Among the Partners

Goodman Group announced on 16 September the completion of a US$455 million equity raise for the Goodman Hong Kong Data Centre Partnership, with proceeds primarily funding the mechanical and electrical fit out of HKG10, a 50 megawatt data center in Tsuen Wan due to open in early 2028. Capital was provided by a group of institutional and sovereign wealth investors including PGGM, APG, Canada Pension Plan Investment Board and CBRE Investment Management, and once HKG10 opens it will bring the partnership's completed Hong Kong portfolio to 275 megawatts.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  16 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

California Governor Newsom Signs Seven Data Center Bills Creating the Nation's Most Comprehensive Regulatory Framework, Covering Electricity Cost Allocation, Water Disclosure, Environmental Review and a Mandatory Operator Registry

Governor Newsom signed seven data center bills on 21 September covering electricity rate structures intended to prevent grid costs from being passed on to other ratepayers, mandatory water demand disclosure for new developments, an expanded environmental review process under the California Environmental Quality Act and a new statewide operator registry run by the California Energy Commission. The package is described by the Governor's office as the most comprehensive data center legislation enacted by any US state and is widely expected to become a template for other states confronting the land, energy and water demands of AI infrastructure growth.

Source: National Law Review  ·  22 September 2026
 Market Intelligence

Texas Governor Abbott Extends Data Center Permitting Pause to Cover All Environmental Approvals Until ERCOT Completes Its Grid Audit, With Developers Required to Submit Demand and Water Data by 12 October

Governor Greg Abbott issued a directive on 21 September extending an existing moratorium on data center grid interconnection applications to cover all environmental permits and water related approvals administered by Texas state agencies, with ERCOT due to publish its findings by 10 December. Developers with projects in ERCOT's interconnection queue must submit information on projected electricity and water consumption, on site generation, cooling technology and financial incentives by 12 October, and no state agency may proceed with any data center regulatory approval until the audit is complete.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  22 September 2026

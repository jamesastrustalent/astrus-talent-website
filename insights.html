Market Intelligence

Oracle Opens New Data Centers in the UAE and Saudi Arabia as AI Cloud Demand Surges, With More Sites Planned in the Next Six Months

Oracle has added new data center locations in the UAE and Saudi Arabia over the past six months and plans further sites in the next six, a senior executive confirmed to The National on 29 October 2026. The company declines to disclose specific locations after a number of its Gulf facilities were previously targeted by Iran, though it confirmed its UAE capacity has grown substantially to support rising AI workloads.

Source: The National  ·  29 October 2026
 New Builds

UK Founded Developer Yamna Signs a Cooperation Agreement With Oman to Build a Data Center in Salalah With a First Phase Expected to Exceed £1 Billion, as Oman Secures Two AI Compute Deals in One Week

Yamna, a UK founded developer, signed a cooperation agreement with Oman's Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology for the Salalah Al Khadra Digital project, whose first phase is expected to cost more than £1 billion, the Oman Observer reported on 23 October 2026. Oman's position as a Gulf AI compute destination was further confirmed the same week when a separate consortium led by Mubbadrah Investment announced plans for a $3 billion AI computing complex targeting up to 400 MW in the same city.

Source: Oman Observer  ·  23 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Finnish Regulators Order Google to Suspend Work at Two Data Center Sites After Around 250,000 Trees Were Cleared at One Location Before Environmental Impact Assessments Were Completed

Finland's Licensing and Supervision Authority ordered Google's subsidiary Tuike Finland to pause all environmentally significant preparatory work at sites in Muhos and Kajaani until required environmental impact assessments are finalised, Data Center Dynamics reported on 26 October 2026. Google, whose Finnish data center programme is part of a €13 billion investment commitment, acknowledged it had fallen short of its own standards and announced plans to replant trees across 130 hectares near Muhos.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  26 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Microsoft Exits Nscale's £2 Billion AI Data Center Project in Essex After Grid Power Delivery Is Delayed to the 2030s Due to a London Wide Network Constraint

Microsoft has withdrawn from a planned AI data center in Loughton, Essex, developed by UK startup Nscale, after the grid connection required to power the facility was pushed back to the 2030s by what the operator described as a London wide infrastructure issue, the BBC reported on 19 October 2026. Nscale says it remains committed to the site and is exploring alternative power sources, while its broader commercial relationship with Microsoft continues across joint projects in Norway, Portugal and the US.

Source: BBC / TechXplore  ·  19 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Singapore's Parliament Passes the Digital Infrastructure Bill, Introducing Mandatory Licences for Data Centers of 10 MW or More and Fines of Up to 10% of Annual Singapore Turnover for Non Compliance

Singapore's Parliament passed the Digital Infrastructure Bill on 7 October 2026 after a two day debate, creating a licensing regime administered by the IMDA that covers the security, resilience and environmental sustainability of major data centers. Operators of colocation and cloud data centers with at least 10 MW of critical IT load must obtain a licence, and more than 60% of the city state's approximately 70 data centers are expected to be affected when the law comes into force.

Source: Singapore MDDI  ·  7 October 2026

NTT Data Is in Final Talks to Build Anthropic's First Dedicated Data Center in Japan, a 500 MW Facility in Osaka With Costs Estimated at Up to ¥1 Trillion (Approximately US$6.3 Billion)

NTT Data Group is in advanced discussions to build a 500 MW AI data center in Osaka for Anthropic, which would be the AI company's first dedicated facility in Japan and is estimated to cost up to ¥1 trillion (approximately US$6.3 billion), according to a Nikkei report covered by Data Center Dynamics in October 2026. Neither company has confirmed the deal, which the parties reportedly aim to finalise by the end of the Japanese fiscal year, with partial operations targeted for 2028.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  14 October 2026

Voters in 10 US States Will Consider 41 Local Measures on Election Day to Restrict, Delay or Ban New Data Centers, With Ohio Leading the Nation With 18 Communities Voting on Limits

Voters in 38 jurisdictions across 10 states will consider at least 41 measures on 3 November 2026 that would impose new requirements on, pause or outright prohibit data center construction, as a bipartisan backlash against rapid AI driven development grows, the Detroit News reported on 29 October 2026. Ohio leads the nation with 18 communities voting, including Granville, Hubbard and Urbana where residents are considering outright bans; measures elsewhere range from zoning changes to mandatory voter approval requirements.

Source: Detroit News  ·  29 October 2026

Charlotte City Council Votes to Extend Its Data Center Permit Pause by 11 Months as the City Reviews Policy Ahead of the 2027 North Carolina Legislative Session

Charlotte City Council voted on 27 October 2026 to extend the city's data center moratorium by a further 11 months, keeping in place a pause on new permit approvals first introduced in June 2026, WUNC reported. Projects that submitted full applications before the original pause took effect on 8 June are not affected, and the council is counting on North Carolina lawmakers to pass new data center regulations early in the 2027 legislative session.

Source: WUNC / NPR  ·  27 October 2026

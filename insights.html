Gulf States Press Ahead with AI Data Center Build Even as Regional Conflict Targets Facilities

State backed firms in the United Arab Emirates have continued construction on a planned five gigawatt AI campus in Abu Dhabi, with the first 200 megawatt Stargate UAE cluster remaining on course to come online in the third quarter of 2026, after additional worker safety measures were introduced following drone strikes that damaged Amazon data center facilities in the UAE and Bahrain. Bloomberg reports that Gulf governments are treating the security environment as a catalyst to accelerate their sovereign AI infrastructure programmes rather than scale them back.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  4 September 2026
 New Builds

Microsoft Confirms Saudi Arabia East Azure Region Will Be Available to Customers in November 2026

Microsoft announced at the LEAP 2026 technology event in Riyadh on 31 August that its Saudi Arabia East data center region, located in the Eastern Province and comprising three Azure Availability Zones, will open to enterprise and government customers in November 2026. The region will allow organisations across the Kingdom to access cloud and AI services with local data residency and low latency connectivity, in support of Saudi Vision 2030's digital economy ambitions.

Source: Microsoft  ·  31 August 2026
 Capex Watch

SoftBank Pledges €75 Billion to Develop 5GW of AI Data Center Capacity Across France

SoftBank Group announced at the Choose France 2026 summit a commitment to develop and operate five gigawatts of AI data center capacity in France, representing a total investment of up to €75 billion and the group's largest single country infrastructure commitment in Europe. The first phase comprises €45 billion for 3.1GW in northern France, with initial sites planned at Dunkirk, Bosquel and Bouchain and the full portfolio to be powered by nuclear and renewable energy.

Source: Bloomberg  ·  30 May 2026
 Market Intelligence

Power Constraints Are Pushing Europe's Data Center Pipeline Beyond Traditional Hubs as AI Investment Heads Toward €176 Billion

JLL's EMEA data center mid year 2026 review finds that power availability has become the primary barrier to growth across Europe's established colocation markets, with new projects planned between 2026 and 2028 now located an average of 175 kilometres from major hub cities, versus 46 kilometres for campuses delivered between 2022 and 2025. The European Data Centre Association forecasts cumulative investment of €176 billion across the region from 2026 to 2031, with the Nordics and Tier 2 markets such as Madrid and Milan expected to absorb more than half of all AI capacity growth.

Source: JLL  ·  2026
 Capex Watch

Amazon Pledges an Additional $13 Billion for AI and Cloud Data Centers in India, Lifting Total Commitment to $48 Billion

Amazon announced on 25 June that it will invest an additional $13 billion to expand AWS data center capacity across Mumbai and Hyderabad, taking its total committed investment in India to $48 billion between 2026 and 2030, in an announcement made during a meeting between CEO Andy Jassy and Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi. The additional funding will accelerate access to AI infrastructure, custom chips and managed cloud services for enterprises, startups and government organisations across the country.

Source: CNBC  ·  25 June 2026
 Market Intelligence

Blackstone Commits $30 Billion to AI Data Centers in Japan over Three to Five Years as AirTrunk Scales Toward 1GW

Blackstone President Jonathan Gray told Nikkei Asia that the firm plans to invest $30 billion in AI data center infrastructure across Japan over the next three to five years through its AirTrunk platform, building on more than 500 megawatts of capacity already in operation in the country. Gray framed the commitment as a conviction bet on accelerating AI demand, arguing that the risk of building too little compute capacity substantially outweighs the risk of overbuilding.

Source: Nikkei Asia  ·  June 2026
 New Builds

OpenAI Unveils Project Camellia: a $20 Billion AI Data Center Campus in Effingham County, Georgia Targeting 3.2GW of Power

OpenAI has announced Project Camellia, a campus spanning approximately 1,400 acres in Effingham County near Savannah, Georgia, with at least $20 billion disclosed in initial investment and a total project value reported above $30 billion, making it the single largest planned electricity user in the state. The facility is planned as four buildings to be powered by Georgia Power and targeted to receive up to 3.2 gigawatts of electricity in phases between 2028 and 2032, with around 400 permanent roles and an $80 million community fund to support local schools, healthcare and public safety.

Source: OpenAI  ·  22 July 2026
 Market Intelligence

Data Center Backlash Becomes September Political Surprise as US Opposition Blocks $130 Billion in Projects

Community opposition to data center development blocked or delayed approximately 75 projects worth $130 billion in planned US construction in the first quarter of 2026 alone, the most in any three month period on record, as the number of active opposition groups more than doubled to 833 across 49 states, according to Data Center Watch research. RealClearPolitics reports the backlash has become a live issue heading into the November 2026 midterm elections, with a Gallup poll finding 70 percent of Americans would oppose a data center being built in their local area, cutting across traditional party lines.

Source: RealClearPolitics  ·  3 September 2026

EQT in Talks With Sovereign Investors in Saudi Arabia and the UAE on Joint AI and Data Center Deals as Swedish Firm Seeks to Extend Its Gulf Footprint

EQT, the Swedish private equity group that operates more than 90 data centers globally through its EdgeConneX platform, is in discussions with sovereign wealth funds in Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates about pursuing joint AI and data center investment deals, as it looks to establish a Gulf presence alongside the region's rapidly expanding infrastructure market. The National reported the development exclusively on 3 November 2026.

Source: The National  ·  3 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

GCC Data Center Investment Surpasses $93 Billion Across More Than 174 Active Projects as Saudi Arabia and the UAE Lead Regional AI Infrastructure Growth

Analysis from MEED Insight shows the total value of active and planned data center projects across the six Gulf Cooperation Council states has exceeded $93 billion across more than 174 schemes, with Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates together accounting for the majority of capacity additions as international operators including AWS and Google build alongside regional developers in a market reshaping the Gulf economy around AI infrastructure.

Source: Trade Arabia / MEED Insight  ·  2026
 Market Intelligence

UK Chancellor Introduces Data Centre Covenant at Autumn Budget Offering Priority Grid Access to Operators That Commit to Local Investment and Environmental Standards

Chancellor John Healey announced the Data Centre Covenant at the Autumn Budget on 28 October 2026, offering data center operators faster access to the electricity grid in return for commitments to invest locally, protect water supplies and meet environmental requirements; a new Ministerial Control Centre will oversee planning and grid approvals for strategic projects, with ministers reserving future grid capacity and facilitating reassignment of grid connection contracts to qualifying schemes.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  28 October 2026
 Market Intelligence

Scottish Government Backs Temporary Halt on Approvals for New Hyperscale Data Centers Above 50 MW Pending Finalisation of National Planning Guidelines

Scottish ministers confirmed in September 2026 that they would not approve any new hyperscale data center schemes until new national planning guidelines are in place, following a vote in the Scottish Parliament; the government also committed to introduce legislation requiring a full environmental impact assessment from any developer seeking to build a large data center in Scotland, as public concern over energy consumption and water use mounted across several communities near proposed sites.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  September 2026
 New Builds

Samsung SDS Breaks Ground on Its First AI Data Center in Gumi, South Korea, With an Initial Capacity of 60 MW Designed to Expand to 120 MW

Samsung SDS held a groundbreaking ceremony on 2 November 2026 at the former Samsung Electronics plant site in Gumi's national industrial complex, marking the first major construction milestone of the company's 60 trillion won Yeongnam investment plan; the facility will begin with 60 MW of capacity and is designed to expand to 120 MW when building work reaches completion in 2028.

Source: Korea Herald  ·  2 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

Australia's Data Center Development Pipeline Reaches 26.8 GW as Melbourne Overtakes Sydney With 10.7 GW of Planned Capacity for the First Time

A joint report from Data Centres Australia and DC Byte published in October 2026 shows the national development pipeline grew by 5.2 GW over six months to reach 26.8 GW, with Melbourne's planned pipeline rising by 1.7 GW to 10.7 GW and surpassing Sydney's 7.8 GW for the first time; the Victorian government simultaneously announced new policy requirements for data centers ahead of the November 2026 state election, alongside a pause on new approvals.

Source: Data Centres Australia / DC Byte  ·  October 2026

Candidates Who Campaigned Against Data Center Development Win Governorships and Legislative Seats Across the United States in the 2026 Midterm Elections

The 2026 US midterm elections returned candidates who had campaigned on restricting data center development, with Democrat Gina Hinojosa winning the Texas governor's race on a pledge of a moratorium on new permits and Democrats winning control of Congress; 38 of 39 nationwide ballot measures on data center restrictions enacted new limits, and AI data center stocks fell sharply on 4 November as investors weighed the risk of project delays in major buildout markets.

Source: Data Center Dynamics  ·  4 November 2026
 Market Intelligence

OpenAI Launches Grid Protectors Program Pledging to Reduce Electricity Demand at Its Own Data Center Campuses When US Power Grids Are Under Strain

OpenAI announced the Grid Protectors Program on 2 November 2026, committing to agree with utilities on the amount of power each of its owned campuses can shed during periods of high grid demand; under the pilot agreement for its planned Effingham County, Georgia campus, Georgia Power can call on OpenAI to reduce load by up to 1,000 MW when needed, with the utility projecting approximately $950 million in annual customer savings from its wider portfolio of large load agreements starting in 2029.

Source: Axios  ·  2 November 2026

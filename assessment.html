What is Astrus Assessment?

Astrus Assessment is a behavioural and competency assessment platform built for businesses that take hiring seriously. Use it as part of your standard recruitment process to evaluate new hires before they start, or run it as a standalone evaluation to understand the team you already have.

New Hire Assessment

Know exactly who you are bringing in before they start. Scored against twelve competencies specific to their role.

Team Evaluation

Understand the strengths and development areas of your existing team. Identify gaps before they become problems.

Report in Minutes

A full seven-page scored report delivered in minutes. Evidence based and built to inform decisions, not just validate them.

Five Role Specific Assessments

Built for the roles where the right hire makes all the difference.

Sales

Commercial acumen, pipeline management, influence, and personal drive.

Marketing

Brand strategy, commercial acumen, campaign execution, and creativity.

Technology

Technical vision, systems architecture, delivery, and engineering standards.

Operations

Strategic planning, process improvement, risk management, and change.

Leadership

Organisational vision, people leadership, performance, and character.

Our Expertise

Talent for Every Stage

From first site visit to live operations, we find the leaders and specialist talent behind every stage of a data center and AI infrastructure build.

Executive Search

We specialise in executive search and leadership hiring across every stage of the build. Every step needs someone accountable for it, from the development director securing the site to the operations leader keeping it live, and we find the people who own each stage and the leaders who run the whole programme.

01

Develop

Finding the land, securing power and permits, and building the business case that shapes everything after it.

The talentDevelopment leaders who can read a site, win over landowners and utilities, and secure power before anyone else does.

AI campuses: Power is the constraint, so sites are chosen for gigawatt scale grid access.

02

Design

Turning the concept into a buildable facility, and ordering long lead equipment early enough to protect the programme.

The talentDesign leaders and engineers who balance performance, cost and buildability, and make the right calls early.

AI campuses: Far higher rack densities, with liquid cooling designed in from day one.

03

Procure

Choosing contractors and the supply chain, and locking in delivery slots in a market where everything is in short supply.

The talentCommercial leaders with the supplier relationships and negotiating weight to secure equipment and contractors on time.

AI campuses: GPUs, cooling and power equipment on allocation, not off the shelf.

04

Build

Delivering the shell and the electrical and mechanical fit out safely, on time and on budget.

The talentConstruction leaders who run a live site safely and keep several phases on programme at the same time.

AI campuses: Compressed programmes, with capacity live in months, not years.

05

Commission

Testing every system, stage by stage, to prove the facility performs before anything goes live.

The talentSpecialists with the rigour to prove every system, and the confidence to stop a handover that is not ready.

AI campuses: Liquid cooling and high density loads that most teams have never tested.

06

Operate

Handing over to operations, onboarding tenants and keeping uptime high, then planning the next phase.

The talentOperations leaders who build the teams, processes and culture that keep a facility running without interruption.

AI campuses: GPU clusters that need a different kind of operations team.

The Build Lifecycle

From first site visit to live operations, we support every stage.

Executive Search

We specialise in executive search and leadership hiring across every stage of the build. Every step needs someone accountable for it, from the development director securing the site to the operations leader keeping it live, and we find the people who own each stage and the leaders who run the whole programme.

01

Develop

Finding the right land, securing power and permits, and building the business case. The decisions made here set the cost, timeline and capacity of everything that follows.

On AI campusesPower is the constraint, and sites are chosen for gigawatt scale grid access.
02

Design

Turning the concept into a buildable facility across electrical, mechanical, cooling and structure, and ordering the long lead equipment early enough to protect the programme.

On AI campusesRack densities many times higher, with liquid cooling designed in from day one.
03

Procure

Choosing the contractors and supply chain, negotiating terms and locking in delivery slots in a market where equipment and labour are in short supply.

On AI campusesGPUs, cooling and power equipment on allocation, not off the shelf.
04

Build

Delivering the shell and the electrical and mechanical fit out safely, on time and on budget, often across several phases running at once.

On AI campusesCompressed programmes, with capacity live in months, not years.
05

Commission

Testing every system, in stages, up to a full test of the whole facility, to prove it performs before anything goes live.

On AI campusesTesting liquid cooling and high density loads that most teams have never run.
06

Operate

Handing over to the operations team, onboarding tenants and keeping uptime high, then planning the next phase on the campus.

On AI campusesGPU clusters that need a different kind of operations team.

About Us

Specialist Recruiters for Mission-Critical Environments

Astrus Talent is a niche recruitment firm focused exclusively on Data Centers and mission critical infrastructure. We deliver talent globally, supporting hyperscale providers, colocation operators, and construction firms worldwide.

Our Approach

We're not a generalist agency. We understand the technical demands of Data Center environments, the urgency of uptime, and the calibre of people required. We act as a trusted advisor, not a transactional supplier.

Global Delivery

We maintain a global network of relationships across the Data Center industry. Whether you're building in Frankfurt, operating in Virginia, or expanding in the Gulf, we deliver.
